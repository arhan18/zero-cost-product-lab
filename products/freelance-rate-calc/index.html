Why did I get this number?

Billable hours = hours/week × weeks/year × (1 − non-billable%). Needed revenue = (income + expenses) ÷ (1 − tax/buffer%). Minimum hourly = needed revenue ÷ billable hours. Recommended = minimum × 1.15, a negotiation and scope-creep buffer shown separately — delete it mentally if you charge with confidence. Daily = recommended × 8. Project = recommended × your project hours. Arithmetic, not financial advice.
