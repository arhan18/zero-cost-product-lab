# Competitive analysis — Scenica (paraphrased, 2026-09-13 scan)

## Reference (Draftly-class)

Prompt→cinematic site, staged pipeline (plan/shell/sections/assemble), stills + motion clip +
frame-scroll playback, chat/inline edits, publish + ZIP/Git export, credit plans from ~$25/mo
(~593 credits/full site observed). Strengths: complete loop, portable export, MCP/API.
Weaknesses observed: plan-limit contradictions in docs, export gating ambiguity, cost variance
across calculators, trial/auth friction UNKNOWN. Penalties for cloning it: legal + zero
differentiation — hence original product required.

## Landscape (one line each)

- Framer ($10–30/site + seats/add-ons): prompt→canvas→publish, strong CMS/hosting; weak: per-site
  stacking, hosting lock-in, no native 3D pipeline.
- Webflow ($15–25/site + $2,500 Team platform): AI sections/CMS/MCP; weak: complexity, Team price,
  overkill for landing pages.
- Spline ($12–70): real 3D scenes/embeds; weak: not a site builder/CMS/host.
- v0 ($30–100/user): chat→React→Vercel/GitHub; weak: seat+token burn, dev-only, no CMS depth.
- Lovable ($25+ usage): full-stack apps you own; weak: credit unpredictability, backend pauses at zero.
- Bolt ($25–30/member): browser full-stack; weak: token burn scales with project size.
- Dora: most cinematic AI builder — **shutting down May 2026** (dead-but-not-gone demand signal).
- Relume ($14–40+): sitemap→wireframe→Figma/Webflow/React export; weak: needs finishing skill.
- Webstudio ($15–35, open-source): visual + code control + self-host; weak: smaller ecosystem.
- Durable ($25–49): instant SMB sites; weak: design ceiling, no export.
- 10Web ($10–80): WP AI + hosting; weak: WordPress complexity, visit/AI caps.

## Convergence

Prompt→site entry, credit metering + top-ups, preset systems taming randomness, export/Git as
dev trust signal, bundled hosting. NOT competing: cross-page design consistency, surgical edits
with cost ceiling, responsive 3D, generation-time performance budgets, portable SEO-intact export.

## Our wedges (evidenced)

1. Selective element editing + cost ceiling (full-regen burn is priced into every rival).
2. Cinematic Scene System: structured, editable, versioned, responsive-tiered scenes;
   cheapest-effective-medium rule (CSS/SVG/sequence before WebGL).
3. Portability + enforced budgets (clean export, no lock-in, measured targets).
   Dora's shutdown leaves cinematic-native demand with no surviving owner — timing tailwind.
