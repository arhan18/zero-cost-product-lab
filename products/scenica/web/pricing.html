Questions worth answering
before you pay.

What is a credit?

One cent of generation cost. A cinematic site costs ≈106 credits, an edit ≈1, a still ≈4. The builder shows the quote before every expensive job.

What does a full site cost?

Standard 60 cr, Cinematic 106 cr, Maximum 180 cr — plus motion coverage if you animate beyond the hero (+40 or +90 per site). The estimator above uses these exact rates.

What happens if credits run out?

Generation hard-stops with a clear message. Top up with a one-time pack or wait for the monthly reset. Nothing ever overbills.

Do credits reset or roll over?

Plan credits reset monthly and do not roll over. Top-up packs last 12 months.

Do I own the generated site?

Yes. Your brief, content, and export are yours. ZIP contains clean HTML/CSS/JS plus assets and metadata — it opens standalone with no calls to Scenica.

Can I use a custom domain?

On Creator and Studio. Connect it in one step; extra live sites beyond your quota are $5/mo each.

What happens if I cancel?

Sites pause (placeholder), projects and domains are kept. Resubscribe to restore, or export first.

Which plan for heavy cinematic projects?

Studio: 8,000 credits cover ≈20 maximum-quality builds with full motion, plus priority queue. Beyond that, Enterprise caps.

What is included in the trial?

One partial watermarked preview build — the full pipeline taste, no domain, no export, no card required (mocked in this prototype).
