# MASTER RESEARCH SYNTHESIS

Synthesis across the lab's three evidence corpora — **A.** 100 product/website benchmark (`design-benchmarks/`) · **B.** 50 founder benchmark (`founders/`) · **C1.** 100 indie/micro-SaaS, 28-field (`indie-saas/`) · **C2.** 100 indie businesses, 68-field (`indie-business/`). Every conclusion graded: **FACT** (directly evidenced) · **OBSERVATION** (counted in-corpus) · **INFERENCE** (reasoned from evidence) · **HYPOTHESIS** (plausible, unproven) · **UNKNOWN** (unverifiable — preserved, not filled).

## PRODUCT DESIGN

- **OBSERVATION:** 56/100 A-records lead with a real product artifact; one accent color, whitespace-as-system, and boring CTAs ("Start free") recur across buckets.
- **INFERENCE:** Specificity (real UI, real numbers, real customers) is the observable difference between credible and generic pages.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** Motion-led marketing with calm product chrome generalizes beyond the corpus.

## UX

- **OBSERVATION:** Successful products narrow to one task, one page, one price where possible (A10/F6 simplicity pattern; C2 single-feature wedges).
- **INFERENCE:** Reference extraction works at the principle level (hierarchy, IA, interaction) — identity-level copying never appears in winning records.

## PRODUCT DISCOVERY

- **OBSERVATION:** Own-itch and agency-pull dominate C1/C2 origins; audience-first and accidental demand are real but rarer.
- **INFERENCE:** Reachable-first-10-users predicts early traction better than idea novelty.

## VALIDATION

- **OBSERVATION:** Pre-sell, waitlists, concierge-first, AppSumo-as-validation, and open-source adoption all appear with dated evidence; launch spikes almost never validate.
- **INFERENCE:** Paid commitment (even tiny) is the only validation signal that survives contact with the contradiction log.

## MVP

- **FACT:** First versions were routinely trivial (single endpoint, static page, no-code, weekend builds) — documented across C1/C2 MVP reconstructions.
- **INFERENCE:** Deliberately-not-built lists matter more than feature lists for solo survival.

## ENGINEERING

- **OBSERVATION:** Zero microservices in 200 business records; Rails/Django/Laravel/Next/PHP/vanilla dominate; SQLite-first.
- **INFERENCE:** Boring stacks are a survival strategy, not a taste deficit.

## AI USAGE

- **OBSERVATION:** 56/100 C2 records use AI, overwhelmingly as augmentation (copy, transcription, colorization, headshots), not foundation work.
- **FACT:** Unit economics decide AI products (Chatbase metering vs Vibe AI negative margins — both documented).
- **UNKNOWN:** Long-run inference price direction per provider.

## DISTRIBUTION

- **OBSERVATION:** SEO 51–63/100 across corpora; community 46; affiliates, podcasts, integrations, directories recur; paid ads absent day-one everywhere.
- **INFERENCE:** Distribution designed into the artifact (credits, footers, API) beats distribution bolted on afterward.

## SEO

- **OBSERVATION:** Intent-query ownership compounds for years (rank-and-monetize records in all corpora).
- **INFERENCE:** Ownable-query test (Phase-15 gate) filters out most generic SEO plays before money is spent.

## PRICING

- **OBSERVATION:** Subscription default (75/100 C2); $9–$99/mo solo sweet spot; freemium funnels; meter-by-cost-dimension; LTD→recurring paths; quota-priced AI.
- **INFERENCE:** Pricing is where capital structure shows (bootstrapped 0% fees vs funded 5%).

## MONETIZATION

- **OBSERVATION:** Subscriptions, one-time desktop licenses, transaction fees, exits (lempire, Paystone, Jasper, Tithe.ly), LTD events, donations/tips.
- **INFERENCE:** Exit-as-return is a legitimate indie outcome, not a failure to scale.

## RETENTION

- **OBSERVATION:** Recurring value (monitors, schedulers, newsletters, communities) retains; one-shot utilities churn unless they become workflows.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** Maintenance-mode economics (Numerous.ai-class) generalize to lifestyle-scale tools.

## SUPPORT

- **OBSERVATION:** Support burden is the hidden scaling tax (vertical SaaS, AI products, marketplace-adjacent tools); desktop/static tools carry near-zero burden.
- **INFERENCE:** Gate-11 veto exists because of this asymmetry.

## SOLO-MAINTAINABILITY

- **FACT:** Solo-run $3M businesses exist (Photopea-class costs ~$12.6K/yr; Levels portfolio).
- **INFERENCE:** Maintainability = scope discipline + boring stack + metered costs, not heroics.

## FAILURES

- **OBSERVATION:** 20+ failure/decline records: platform-rent repricing, convergence death, negative margins, IP takedowns, raised-and-stalled, sunset-by-acquisition.
- **INFERENCE:** Structural causes dominate executional ones — check structure first in kill arithmetic.

## FOUNDERS

- **OBSERVATION:** B-corpus: distribution skill compounds across products; audience reuse; postmortem generosity; solo decision-making (38/50 solo).
- **UNKNOWN:** Systematic gender/geography gaps persist across all corpora (documented, unfilled).

## $0 FEASIBILITY

- **OBSERVATION:** C2 YES 62 / POSSIBLY 29 / NO 7. Safest: desktop, static, OSS-core, cron, quota-metered AI, BYOK, directories.
- **INFERENCE:** Free-to-prototype ≠ free-to-operate; platform rent reprices without notice (X API case).

## $100 MRR RELEVANCE

- **OBSERVATION:** C2 HIGH 55 / MEDIUM 16 / LOW 15. $10→10, $25→4, $100→1 math holds across records.
- **INFERENCE:** The arithmetic is a sanity check that kills weak ideas early — its purpose is emotional-attachment prevention, not forecasting.
