# Idea Brief — Flat-Rate SMB Support Bot

1. Product: flat-rate AI support chatbot for 1–20-person SMBs (site-trained resolver + human handoff). 2. Category: AI.
3. Why trending: Fin/Salesforce $3.6B deal Jun 2026 validated outcome pricing — and triggered per-resolution backlash ($0.99/resolution billing on silence; $4k→$9k bills).
4. Target user: SMB owners drowning in repetitive pre-sales/support questions, <50 staff. 5. Problem: enterprise agents priced $95–350k/yr minimums; SMBs priced out.
6. Existing products: Intercom Fin, Sierra ($150k min), Decagon ($95k min), Chatbase ($10M ARR solo proof).
7. Clone/wedge: flat $49–99/mo with caps + Shopify/WhatsApp-native, vs per-resolution metering.
8. Distribution: "intercom alternative" SEO, SMB communities, Shopify ecosystem, agencies.
9. MVP: doc-ingest + answer + handoff + quota caps; 2–3 weeks on chatbot APIs.
10. AI margin: manageable — retrieval + small-model answers, hard quotas, no free tier; BYOK option. Grade: INFERENCE (needs per-vertical measurement).
11. Platform risk: LOW (SMB long tail, workflow + handoff owns the relationship).
12. Time-to-MVP: 2–4 weeks. 13. $100 MRR: $49 × 3 customers.
14. Strongest evidence: Fin backlash threads + Chatbase $0→$10M ARR $0 raised (FACT-pattern).
15. Strongest counterevidence: Chatbase already serves this wedge; differentiation must be vertical or channel-specific.
16. Verdict: **BUILD** → Phase 02. Filed 2026-09-12, TRENDING AI mode.
