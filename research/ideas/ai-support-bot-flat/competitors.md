## WHAT CAN WE DO THAT THEY DON'T?

Generic flat bot: nothing evidenced — sub-$99 is crowded floor-to-ceiling and vertical templates already ship. The ONE evidenced opening: ecom order-ACTION depth (WISMO/WISMR/returns executed, not answered) at micro-SMB flat pricing — Gorgias proves buyers pay for actions (99% product-Q elimination, 47% chat conversion at REP AI reference), while its price/complexity excludes the bottom. That is a vertical wedge with FACT backing, not a generic-flat wedge.
