# Customer — Flat-Rate SMB Support Bot

## PRIMARY USER

E-commerce micro-brand owner-operator (1–5 people, Shopify, 50–500 orders/mo) drowning in WISMO/WISMR/returns tickets; or micro-SaaS founder doing support between coding sprints.

## PRIMARY BUYER

Same owner (card-in-hand, sub-$100 decisions without committee).

## SECONDARY USER

Their customers (answer recipients) — experience quality determines retention-by-proxy.

## Profile

- Job-to-be-done: stop answering the same 5 questions without hiring or overpaying.
- Trigger: ticket spike (sale season, shipping delays), a $200+ surprise bill, or a wrong-answer embarrassment.
- Workflow: founder inbox + shared Gmail + WhatsApp tabs + phone for older buyers.
- Frustration: overlaps, >24hr replies, meter anxiety (credits, per-resolution assumptions).
- Desired outcome: questions answered AND orders acted on, humans looped for edge cases.
- Current workaround: free tiers + manual inbox (dominant), VA replies at volume.
- Buying trigger: volume pain or bill shock — reactive.
- Objections: hallucination fear (Air Canada/Cursor/DPD stories travel far), "another $49 subscription," setup/training effort, trust in solo vendor with customer relationships, free-tier adequacy.
- Willingness-to-pay evidence: PROVEN for category ($70–220 stacks, Chatbase $10M ARR); PROVEN for ecom actions (Gorgias pattern); UNPROVEN for generic flat bot specifically and for vertical claims outside ecom.
- Acquisition channel: alternative-SEO + grievance-thread replies + Shopify ecosystem (INFERENCE on engagement).
