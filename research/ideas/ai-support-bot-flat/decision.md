# Decision — Flat-Rate SMB Support Bot

## WHY THIS PRODUCT SHOULD EXIST (candidate, now tested)

Was: flat $49–99 generic bot. After evidence: generic-flat is a crowded floor (dozens sub-$99, incumbents shipping vertical templates + white-label), and "verticalized" is vendor marketing everywhere except ONE place — e-commerce order actions, where buyers demonstrably pay for executed outcomes (WISMO 30–50% of tickets; 99% product-Q elimination reference). "Verticalized" is NOT differentiation until evidenced — and here, in exactly one vertical, it is.

## MVP (pivot-scoped, not to be built yet)

Ecom order-action bot: Shopify-native WISMO/WISMR/returns execution + policy answers with citations + human handoff; $79 flat; quota-capped; retrieval-only with refuse-when-unsure. Explicitly NOT: generic multi-vertical bot, voice, SMS, AI-generated policies.

## $100 MRR TEST

$79 × 2 micro-brands = $158: FEWER customers than generic math, each with proven ticket economics. Reachability via Shopify ecosystem + alternative-SEO (INFERENCE, unobserved conversions). Arithmetic is not validation — pivot validates it.

## SOLO-MAINTAINABILITY

Narrowed scope helps: one platform (Shopify), one ticket family, one integration surface. Remaining burden (per-tenant syncs, stale-policy liability, non-technical support) is MEDIUM, reducible via retrieval-only design + handoff defaults. Better than generic multi-vertical; still the top ongoing risk.

## AI-MARGIN

Headroom confirmed (~$1–35/mo cost at realistic volumes vs $79 price). Deprecation treadmill + token volatility monitored, not blocking. Margin was never the risk.

## KILL CONDITIONS (for the pivot)

No ecom pay evidence after targeted validation (5+ priced-switching or pre-pay signals) · Gorgias downmarket move or Shopify-native solution absorbs the wedge · handoff/escalation expectations exceed solo ops · WISMO automation proves harder than ticket share suggests (pilot failures).

## REVERSAL CONDITIONS (for generic-flat, if pivot fails)

None currently — generic-flat stays KILLED unless a new meter-trap event (e.g. industry-wide per-resolution hike) creates fresh switching demand with evidence.

## DECISION: PIVOT

Problem real (FACT), category spend proven (FACT), but the generic-flat wedge is commoditized with no moat and heavy solo ops. PIVOT to the ecom order-action wedge at $79 flat — same problem family, specified customer (Shopify micro-brands), specified differentiation (executed outcomes + no-trap flat pricing), all with FACT backing. The pivot, not the product, now enters wedge validation.

## EVIDENCE SUMMARY

- PRIMARY: pricing pages (Chatbase, Fin, Tidio, Crisp, Freshworks, Dante/MyAskAI/SiteGPT); Gorgias AI pages + Shopify WISMO data; Air Canada tribunal record (CanLII); sender-requirement docs.
- SECONDARY: G2/Trustpilot distributions; Klarna reversal reporting (Bloomberg); Cursor/DPD incident coverage; IH founder accounts; cost-operator blogs.
- CONTRADICTORY: "flat gap exists" vs no observed flat-switching; "verticalization differentiates" vs proven ONLY in ecom-actions; "huge margins" vs liability/ops eating them; "urgent pain" vs nice-to-have with maxed AI budgets.
- UNKNOWN: flat-tier churn curves; SMS/TCPA viability; grievance-thread conversion rates; exact per-tenant ops load.
