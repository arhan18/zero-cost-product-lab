# Customer — BYOK SDR-lite

## PRIMARY USER

Operator at a 2–10-person done-for-you lead-gen agency running 3–10 B2B SMB client accounts, sending 30–150k/mo, currently stacking Clay + Smartlead/Instantly + verifiers.

## PRIMARY BUYER

Same agency owner (buys tools from margin pain; no committee).

## SECONDARY USER

Solo SEO/marketing agency owners doing own outbound (lower volume, $62–111/mo current stack).

## Profile

- Job-to-be-done: keep per-client outreach profitable as credit/action bills swing.
- Trigger: monthly Clay/Smartlead invoice shock; client threatening churn over lead cost.
- Workflow: Clay → verifiers → Smartlead/Instantly → sheets/CRM; constant repricing math.
- Frustration: unpredictable unit costs, not lack of features.
- Desired outcome: one predictable number per month.
- Current workaround: downgrading tiers, free-tier arbitrage, moving to $25–55 senders.
- Buying trigger: bill spike or client loss — reactive, not proactive.
- Objections: deliverability ("will my inboxing survive a move?"), migration effort, tool sprawl fatigue, trust in a solo vendor with their sending reputation.
- Willingness-to-pay evidence: PROVEN for cheaper sending ($25–55 moves documented); UNPROVEN for $99 BYOK bundle specifically.
- Acquisition channel: r/gtmengineering pricing threads + r/agency replies (INFERENCE on engagement; conversions unobserved).
