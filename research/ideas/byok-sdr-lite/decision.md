# Decision — BYOK SDR-lite

## WHY THIS PRODUCT SHOULD EXIST (candidate, now tested)

Was: flat predictable BYOK bundle for margin-squeezed agencies. After evidence: the differentiation (BYOK) is shipped by incumbents and cited by zero buyers as a purchase reason; the price ($99) sits above where switchers actually land ($25–55). No surviving "why us."

## MVP (not to be built)

Would-have-been: CSV enrich + AI drafts + approval queue + sender integration + quota caps. Kept on record so a future variant doesn't restart from zero.

## $100 MRR TEST

$99 × 2 = $198: ACQUISITION-plausible (two named agency archetypes reachable via thread replies), RETENTION-implausible (deliverability liability + $25–55 gravity + list decay). Arithmetic passes; economics fail. Arithmetic is not validation.

## SOLO-MAINTAINABILITY

FAIL: shared-fate deliverability, abuse desk needs, compliance exposure (CAN-SPAM/GDPR/CASL), unfixable-by-code support load. REDUCE/AUTOMATE cannot remove inbox-reputation responsibility; REPRICE cannot fix a $25–55 market; KILL applies.

## AI-MARGIN

Validated non-issue (BYOK economics sound) — and irrelevant: margin safety without demand or liability cover is not viability. The dangerous assumption was never the AI. Confirmed.

## KILL CONDITIONS (met)

No pricing edge (incumbents already flat/BYOK) · deliverability liability structurally solo-hostile · $99 tier has zero observed switchers · retention cliff (list decay, month-4 plateau churn) · platform dependence (Google caps, enforcement, vendor pricing).

## REVERSAL CONDITIONS

A verified sub-segment paying ≥$99 specifically for predictability/BYOK (5+ priced-switching evidences) · a deliverability architecture a solo founder can actually operate (evidence, not design) · an incumbent exit creating a forced-migration event · a narrower wedge (e.g. verification-only micro-tool with pay evidence — separate brief).

## DECISION: KILL

Evidence does not justify continued work. Real problem (Clay pricing pain, FACT), proven adjacent demand ($25–55 switching, FACT), but this product/customer/wedge has no edge and full liability. Killed with reversal conditions filed — the system distinguished technically-easy from actually-viable, which was the test.

## EVIDENCE SUMMARY

- PRIMARY: pricing pages (Clay, Smartlead, Instantly, MillionVerifier, 11x); Yahoo/Google sender requirements; Word to the Wise ESP guidance; r/gtmengineering revolt + switching threads.
- SECONDARY: teardown blogs, agency stack writeups, review aggregations.
- CONTRADICTORY: "revolt proves $99 demand" vs switchers landing $25–55; "BYOK saves money" (true) vs "BYOK sells" (unevidenced); "$100 MRR easy" vs retention cliff.
- UNKNOWN: exact churn curves; TCPA-SMS viability; any BYOK-premium buyer anywhere.
