## WHAT CAN WE DO THAT THEY DON'T?

Not "more features," not "$3 cheaper." The only unoccupied bundle: flat pricing + auto-updates + unlimited seats/subs + NO white-label/privacy cliff + commercial-use-safe positioning for UptimeRobot free-tier refugees. No single incumbent owns all five — but none of the five alone has proven switching demand, so this is INFERENCE, not FACT.
