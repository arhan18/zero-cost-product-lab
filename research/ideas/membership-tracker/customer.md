# Customer — Membership/Service-Agreement Tracker

## PRIMARY USER

Owner/office manager at a 4-tech-scale HVAC/plumbing/pest shop with 100–300 agreements, on QuickBooks + spreadsheets, pre-Titan by their own stated threshold (4 vans + 300–500 agreements).

## PRIMARY BUYER

Same owner (card-in-hand; suite purchases are committee-scale, tracker purchases are impulse-scale — which cuts both ways for retention).

## SECONDARY USER

Techs performing agreement tune-up visits (passive beneficiaries).

## Profile

- Job-to-be-done: never miss a renewal or seasonal visit across a growing member base.
- Trigger: Sunday-night spreadsheet dread; a lapsed best-customer discovery; spring/fall scheduling crunch.
- Workflow: Excel anniversary log + Calendar repeats + 30/7-day chase calls/texts + QuickBooks billing.
- Frustration: calendar glitches, slipped follow-ups, growth outpacing memory ("worked until it didn't" at ~120–150 agreements).
- Desired outcome: renewals + visits happen without the spreadsheet.
- Current workaround: Excel + Calendar + phone (dominant); suite upgrade at 4 vans (stated threshold).
- Buying trigger: NONE OBSERVED for standalone (UNKNOWN — the gap).
- Objections: 4th-system fatigue; migration of member/payment/visit history; "will I open it enough to justify $49?"; trust for billing data in a tiny vendor.
- WTP evidence: WEAK — price complaints about suites exist; zero priced standalone purchases observed.
- Acquisition channel: trade subs + SEO (reach INFERENCE, conversion UNKNOWN).
