# Decision — Membership/Service-Agreement Tracker

## WHY IT SHOULD EXIST (candidate, now tested)

Was: $49 tracker for pre-Titan shops drowning in Excel. After evidence: pain real but narrow (100–300 agreements), frequency physics hostile (2×/year opens), willingness is for cheaper-FSM-replacement not add-on tracker, and a 4th system gets explicitly rejected. No surviving "why us" as a standalone.

## MVP (not to be built)

Would-have-been: member import + anniversary engine + 30/7-day chase + seasonal visit scheduling + QuickBooks billing sync. Kept on record.

## $100 MRR TEST

$49 × 3 = $147: one HVAC (spring tune-ups), one plumbing (annual inspections), one cleaning (monthly plans) — each already on QB, each wanting expiry/visit tracking without $329 Max. Plausible archetypes, zero observed buyers. Arithmetic is not validation.

## SOLO-MAINTAINABILITY

Buildable, operable — but churn, not ops, is the killer: a twice-yearly-opened product cannot hold $49/mo subscriptions. REDUCE (to a feature inside a daily-use product) is the only honest direction; standalone fails the retention gate.

## AI-MARGIN

NOT APPLICABLE (no AI core).

## KILL CONDITIONS (met)

Frequency physics (tax-software churn at $49) · 4th-system rejection (verbatim) · no standalone WTP observed · trivial incumbent response (recurring billing + reminders already shipped) · retention requires becoming billing system of record (ungrantable to a tracker).

## REVERSAL CONDITIONS

3 prepaid $49 pilots from QB+spreadsheet micro-shops with <50 agreements (the evidence agent's own bar) · measured 12-month retention on a tracker cohort · a suite exit/abandonment event pushing micro-shops out with switching evidence.

## DECISION: KILL

Why it died: the central question — recurring workflow painful enough to OWN — failed on frequency (2×/year opens), ownership (4th-system rejection), and WTP (complaints about suites, zero standalone purchases). Strongest evidence: the pre-Titan threshold quotes + LevelCFO renewal economics (real pain, wrong product shape). Strongest contradiction: "driven up the wall" vs twice-yearly usage. Next gate: none. Remaining unknowns: sub-50-agreement micro-shop WTP (untested pocket, insufficient to sustain INVESTIGATE alone against structural frequency physics).

## EVIDENCE SUMMARY

- PRIMARY: official pricing pages (ServiceTitan, HCP, Jobber, FieldPulse, Kickserv); r/ProHVACR, r/smallbusiness, r/pestcontrol threads (verbatim pain + thresholds).
- SECONDARY: LevelCFO benchmarks; BuiltOnTenth research; field-service switching guides.
- CONTRADICTORY: real seasonal pain vs 2×/year usage; $329 hate vs keeping suites; plausible archetypes vs zero buyers.
- UNKNOWN: sub-50 WTP; Reddit-side switching quotes (fetch-blocked this pass, flagged).
