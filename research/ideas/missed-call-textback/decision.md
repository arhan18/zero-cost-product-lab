# Decision — Missed-Call Textback

## WHY IT SHOULD EXIST (candidate, now tested)

Was: $49 instant text-back + booking link for micro-shops underserved by suites. After evidence: the underserved premise is false — Jobber ships it at $29, HCP at $59, GHL at $97, Twilio DIY under $5. No surviving "why us."

## MVP (not to be built)

Would-have-been: number connect + instant SMS + booking link + after-hours rules. Kept on record.

## $100 MRR TEST

$49 × 3 = $147: arithmetic passes; acquisition unproven (no switching evidence) AND retention hostile (consolidation into FSM, 10DLC friction, spam flags). Arithmetic is not validation.

## SOLO-MAINTAINABILITY

FAIL-LEANING: A2P 10DLC vendor + per-shop registration support, porting support, deliverability blame, non-technical owners with their business number at stake.

## AI-MARGIN

NOT APPLICABLE (no AI core).

## KILL CONDITIONS (met)

Checkbox-not-hole (every payer owns it) · zero observed switching into standalone · 10DLC burden structurally solo-hostile · unattributable value · incumbent 1-sprint response already executed by Jobber.

## REVERSAL CONDITIONS

Carrier-rule change removing per-shop 10DLC burden with evidence · a verified exodus from suites over text-back specifically (5+ priced-switching evidences) · a sub-segment without suite coverage and with pay proof (separate brief).

## DECISION: KILL

Why it died: the central question — pay + switch + attributable value — failed on all three despite real pain. Strongest evidence: suite pricing pages proving universal bundling. Strongest contradiction: "$49 cheap" vs "$0 native." Next gate: none. Remaining unknowns: churn drivers (429-blocked re-run flagged if ever revived).

## EVIDENCE SUMMARY

- PRIMARY: official pricing pages (Jobber, HCP, GHL, Podium, Birdeye, Twilio); carrier/A2P docs; GHL porting guides; trade review roundups.
- SECONDARY: r/Plumbing, r/smallbusiness, r/automation threads; vendor studies (treated as INFERENCE, vendor-sourced).
- CONTRADICTORY: vendor $1,200/call stats vs deliberate-voicemail owners; "cheap at $49" vs "$0 native."
- UNKNOWN: exact churn drivers; SMS-deliverability variance by market.
