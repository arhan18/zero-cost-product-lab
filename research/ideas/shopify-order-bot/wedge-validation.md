# Wedge Validation — Shopify Micro-Brand Order Bot

Prior: `../ai-support-bot-flat/decision.md` verdict PIVOT (generic-flat commoditized). This file tests the new wedge. Originals untouched. Method: PUBLIC DEMAND VALIDATION ONLY (no outreach; see `outreach.md`).

## Definitions

- ORIGINAL PRODUCT IDEA: flat $49–99 generic AI support bot for 1–20-person SMBs.
- REAL PROBLEM: repetitive order-support load (WISMO #1 ticket class; copy-paste answering; back-and-forth without order numbers).
- NEW WEDGE: $79/mo flat Shopify-only bot for 1–5-person / 50–500-orders-mo micro-brands: order-aware answers + safe gated actions, no per-resolution/credit traps, commercial-safe positioning.
- TARGET CUSTOMER: solo/2–5-person Shopify micro-brand operators (modal r/shopify profile: 2–5 orders/day, $3–5k/mo).
- TRIGGER EVENT: WISMO ticket pileup; Gorgias/metered bill shock; UptimeRobot-class ToS-style push (none observed here).
- CURRENT ALTERNATIVES: Shopify Inbox free + Magic + Sidekick; Gmail/manual; Tidio/Crisp free tiers; Gorgias Starter $10/Basic $60; Chatbase $40+; part-time PH VA $7–10/hr.
- WHY THE WEDGE SHOULD MATTER: flat predictability + order-aware execution where free tools stop and enterprise tiers start.
- WHAT MUST BE TRUE: WISMO load is real at micro scale; flat drives purchase (not just complaints); order-aware beats generic in buying decisions; trust extends to unknown vendors; Shopify-only keeps solo ops viable; $79 clears budgets; incumbents/free first-party leave room.

## Hypotheses (tested below; full evidence in `pivot-result.md`)

- H1 WISMO workload real at micro scale: SUPPORTED (18% floor Gorgias dataset; 70%-of-tickets anecdote; copy-paste pain threads).
- H2 1–5 / 50–500 segment real + distinct: SUPPORTED (modal profile; flat/free preference; VA hiring specs).
- H3 flat pricing drives purchase: WEAK (complaints abundant; only purchase driver = one Crisp quote; Gorgias ticket-model also wins buys).
- H4 order-aware > generic in buying: SUPPORTED (Casely −76% WISMO $1880/mo; ALOHAS 56%; "can't pull order = won't remove load").
- H5 narrow system trusted for low-risk workflows: SUPPORTED WITH CONDITIONS (read-only-first + gated actions + handoff table-stakes — 89% demand human option; Air Canada/Cursor/Priceline precedents).
- H6 Shopify-only aids solo-maintainability: WEAK-HELP (one API/review pipeline; real cost is review + trust; fees 0% to $1M then 15% + 2.9%).
- H7 $79 plausible: SUPPORTED WITH CAVEAT (undercuts $100–260 AI stacks; exceeds $0–60 bare spend; tiny-store norm is free-first).
- H8 incumbents can't easily kill it: FAILED → THREAT (Gorgias $10 Starter downmarket; Tidio owns micro-SMB; Chatbase native Shopify Feb 2026; Inbox/Magic/Sidekick free and moving toward auto-resolve).
- H9 profitable heavy use: SUPPORTED ON COSTS ($0.05–0.30 marginal/ticket; 50 tickets ≈ $3–15; 300 ≈ $15–90 → needs quotas past ~150; heavy 500+ needs overage/top-ups). Economics work; pricing power doesn't.

## Read-only vs autonomous (resolved)

READ-ONLY-FIRST + GATED ACTIONS + HANDOFF wins on evidence (merchant demands: cited answers, approvals, caps, audit logs; competitor pattern: Gorgias conditions + confirmations, Tidio scoped authenticated calls). Autonomous refunds/edits without gates: rejected by liability precedents.
