# Decision — Outlier Thumbnail Engine

## WHY THIS PRODUCT SHOULD EXIST (candidate, now tested)

Was: $29 niche outlier/title tool undercutting $89. After evidence: the undercut already happened (1of10 $69, Basic $29), the jobs are free elsewhere (ViewStats discovery, native validation), and no niche carries pay evidence. No surviving "why us."

## MVP (not to be built)

Would-have-been: outlier scan + title variants + thumbnail briefs on public view data with weekly pipeline UX. Kept on record so a future variant doesn't restart from zero.

## $100 MRR TEST

$29 × 4 = $116: four named archetypes exist (faceless-automation op, niche coach, agency junior, summit attendee) but all are reachable only via educator affiliates or grind, with no conversion evidence. Arithmetic passes; acquisition does not validate.

## SOLO-MAINTAINABILITY

FAIL-LEANING: pipeline caching, scraping-against-ToS as normal ops, quota-extension roulette, non-technical support load. Reducible in theory (drop scraping, shrink coverage) — but each reduction also deletes the differentiation. Burden and wedge shrink together.

## AI-MARGIN

Validated non-issue (~$1–2/user/mo). Never the risk here.

## KILL CONDITIONS (met)

Occupied price point ($29 = 1of10 Basic) · overnight copyability from shared stack · structural free compression on both jobs · structural retention failure (churn-after-learning; monthly uploaders told not to pay) · HIGH platform risk (quota caps, no paid tier, ToS scraping exposure) · heavy solo ops that shrink with the wedge.

## REVERSAL CONDITIONS

Measured pay evidence in a specific niche (5+ priced-switching or pre-pay evidences, e.g. non-English creators or podcasters) · a distribution edge with conversion proof (educator partnership with rev-share terms) · a YouTube API quota path that removes platform risk (documented, not hoped) · a wedge that survives the free-compression test (job neither ViewStats nor native testing does free).

## DECISION: KILL

Evidence does not justify continued work. Adjacent proven demand ($69–89 tier, FACT) makes this the interesting kind of kill: close enough to be tempting, structurally unwinnable for a solo $0 entrant. No PIVOT specified because no candidate wedge carries pay evidence — inventing one ("non-English!") would be hope, and this system kills hope with filing cabinets.

## EVIDENCE SUMMARY

- PRIMARY: pricing pages (1of10, vidIQ, ViewStats, TubeBuddy, native Test & Compare docs); 1of10's own review/qualification content; Spotter shutdown notice (Tubefilter).
- SECONDARY: r/PartneredYoutube, r/NewTubers, r/youtube, r/ColinAndSamir threads (verbatim); designer-rate threads; OutlierKit/Vidanalyze comparison pages.
- CONTRADICTORY: "outlier demand proven" vs demand accruing to free surfaces; "$29 undercuts" vs $29 already occupied; "niche wedge" vs identical free-compression physics per niche.
- UNKNOWN: 1of10 churn curve; non-English/podcaster pay depth; educator rev-share terms; quota-extension outcomes.
