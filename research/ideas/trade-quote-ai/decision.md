# Decision — Trade Quote/SOW AI

## WHY IT SHOULD EXIST (candidate, now tested)

Was: $49–99 AI quote drafts for trades with $0.05 margins. After evidence: margins confirmed GREEN and wide — and demand absent. Suite natives ship drafting to the installed base; no standalone buyer exists on record. Cost advantage without a buyer is not a business.

## MVP (not to be built)

Would-have-been: call-note/voice → scope extraction → exclusions → quote/SOW draft → review-and-send. Kept on record.

## $100 MRR TEST

$49 × 3 = $147: cannot name 3 shops, reach path, or stay-reason from public demand. FAILED as pipeline (hypothesis only).

## SOLO-MAINTAINABILITY

Buildable, operable — not the binding constraint. Thrown weight at the wrong wall would be the failure mode here, not ops overload.

## AI-MARGIN (INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED — card NOT assumed)

Bands: $0.05–0.50/quote by tier; heavy user (80/mo) $4–40/mo vs $49+ price → GREEN with wide headroom. Credits/quotas/BYOK available but unnecessary at these ratios. Verdict: economics PASS decisively — and still irrelevant, because no buyer exists. The framework's core lesson, demonstrated cleanly: MARGIN ≠ DEMAND.

## KILL CONDITIONS (met)

Zero standalone WTP observed (bundled-only) · suite natives shipping to installed base (oxygen gone) · feature-not-moat (<1-quarter replication) · learned-and-churned retention physics · unnamed $100 MRR pipeline · key per-shop metrics UNKNOWN after good-faith retrieval (flagged, not filled).

## REVERSAL CONDITIONS

5+ priced standalone purchases (or pre-pays) for AI quoting specifically · a suite exit/abandonment event in quoting features with switching evidence · measured per-shop quoting hours + close-rate lift establishing an ROI story a standalone can sell against · a defensible data asset (e.g. trade-specific pricebook network) with pay evidence, separate brief.

## DECISION: KILL

Why it died: the central question — measurable economic value AND unit economics — split exactly wrong: value unmeasured (hours/lift UNKNOWN), economics excellent, buyers absent. Strongest evidence: suite feature pages proving native coverage + pricing pages proving $0-marginal drafting. Strongest contradiction: GREEN margins vs zero buyers. Next gate: none. Remaining unknowns: per-shop quoting hours/close-rate lift; indie saturation count; recording behavior; churn curves (retrieval-flagged).

## EVIDENCE SUMMARY

- PRIMARY: vendor pricing/feature pages (Housecall AI Team/pricing/estimating, Jobber AI/pricing/receptionist); STT/LLM pricing pages (Deepgram, OpenAI); trade press surveys (vendor-sourced → INFERENCE-grade).
- SECONDARY: trade-software roundups; AI-dev cost blogs.
- CONTRADICTORY: GREEN margins vs zero buyers; "viral trade-AI demand" vs suite-accruing demand; "$49 × 3 easy" vs unnamed pipeline.
- UNKNOWN: per-shop hours/lift; indie saturation; recording behavior; churn (search-throttled this pass, flagged honestly).
