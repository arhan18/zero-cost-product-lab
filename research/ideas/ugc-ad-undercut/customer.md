# Customer — UGC Ad Undercut

## PRIMARY USER

DTC micro-brand owner/media buyer (dropship, skincare) testing 10–25 variants/week, spending on creative while watching ROAS daily.

## PRIMARY BUYER

Same operator (card-in-hand; sub-$100 decisions fast, cancellations equally fast).

## SECONDARY USER

Freelance editors/agencies assembling client variants (price-sensitive, tool-hopping).

## Profile

- Job-to-be-done: feed the ad account fresh creative before fatigue (median 11 days) kills ROAS.
- Trigger: CPA spike; creative exhaustion; Arcads bill shock.
- Workflow: creator brief → footage → edit → test → kill/scale (above).
- Frustration: per-video cost at volume; credit burn on failed renders; opaque pricing.
- Desired outcome: winning ads (ROAS), not videos — an OUTCOME buy, which AI wrappers repeatedly fail (3.1% vs 3.7% hooks; revert-to-human).
- Current workaround: creator bundles, $9-tier tools, free tiers, in-house CapCut.
- Buying trigger: a test showing AI variants holding ROAS at lower cost — never observed for a new entrant.
- Objections: AI-disclosure reach penalty; uncanny actors; failed-render billing; cancel-loop stories; "another $29 subscription."
- Willingness-to-pay evidence: PROVEN for creative spend ($150–800 human assets); UNPROVEN for a new $29 AI wrapper specifically.
- Acquisition channel: PPC/ecom subs + alternative-SEO (reach proven; conversion unobserved).
