# Decision — UGC Ad Undercut

## WHY THIS PRODUCT SHOULD EXIST (candidate, now tested)

Was: $29 undercut of Arcads' $11/video. After evidence: the undercut already happened industry-wide ($9–24 floor + free tiers), and buyers pay for ROAS outcomes humans still win. No surviving "why us."

## AI-ECONOMICS-PROOF (LIGHT / EXPECTED / HEAVY / EXTREME at $29 sub)

Assumptions (2026 verified bands): per-ad AI $1.50 LOW / $2.50 expected / $4.00 heavy-mix; payment fees ~3.5%; infra/email ~$2–5/mo share; support allowance $5; safety reserve $3.

- LIGHT (5 ads): revenue $29 − fees $1 − AI $7.50 − other $10 = CONTRIBUTION ~+$10. GREEN.
- EXPECTED (12 ads): $29 − $1 − $30 − $10 = −$12. RED without quotas.
- HEAVY (40 ads): $29 − $1 − $100+ − $10 = −$80+. RED.
- EXTREME (200 ads/abuse): −$300+. Contained ONLY by hard quotas + overage/top-ups.

$49 plan: expected 12 ads → $49 − $1.70 − $30 − $10 = +$7 marginal YELLOW (needs caps). $79 plan: expected → +$37 GREEN; heavy 40 → −$33 RED without overage. Credits + quotas + top-ups make every tier arithmetically survivable — but credits/top-ups/BYOK serve US (margin), not the customer: buyers face meter anxiety (the exact grievance), and BYOK is technically useless (cost concentrates vendor-side). Customer-useful? Quotas yes (predictability); top-ups meh; BYOK no.

Verdict on economics: SURVIVABLE WITH CONTROLS at $49–79, not at $29 flat. But survivable ≠ demanded.

## MVP (not to be built)

Would-have-been: script templates + avatar-API renders + editor + quota ledger. Kept on record.

## $100 MRR TEST

$29 × 4 = $116 is revenue arithmetic, NOT economics (expected-use contribution is negative at $29 flat) and NOT demand (zero observed switching into new entrants). Rejected on both counts — exactly the discipline this framework exists for.

## SOLO-MAINTAINABILITY

THREAT: render ops, failed-render disputes, template support, non-technical sellers, GPU/API billing black boxes.

## KILL CONDITIONS (met)

Occupied floor ($9–24 + free) · no observed down-switching · batch-and-churn retention physics · outcome gap (AI hooks lose to human) · policy/labeling suppression exposure · likeness/IP adjacency · solo ops burden · margin survivable only with the meters buyers hate.

## REVERSAL CONDITIONS

Observed switching into a new sub-$30 entrant (5+ priced evidences) · a render-cost step-change with receipts (5x cheaper verified operator math) · a niche with measured pay evidence (e.g. real-estate video at volume, separate brief) · policy reversal reducing disclosure penalty with measured reach data.

## DECISION: KILL

Economics conditionally survivable, demand structurally absent. Killed with reversal conditions filed.
