- **url**: https://hypefury.com

- **coreProblem**: Creators can't consistently schedule threads or convert viral attention into followers and revenue.
- **productSolution**: X-first scheduler with growth automation (evergreen reposts, auto-plugs, auto-DMs, engagement builder) plus cross-posting.
- **coreFeature**: Thread scheduling + auto-retweet/evergreen recycling with auto-plugs under viral tweets.
- **initialMVP**: Thread-scheduling-only closed beta built in a couple of days after Samy's tweet; invitees were people who replied to the tweet.
- **featuresNotInMVP**: Evergreen recycling, auto-plugs, auto-DMs, engagement builder, analytics, cross-posting, AI trained on user posts, Instagram/LinkedIn support.
- **pricingModel**: Paid subscription, 7-day free trial, no free plan.

- **firstUserAcquisition**: People who replied to the founding tweet were invited to closed beta; Samy did up to 2-hour demo calls with early users.
- **first100Users**: Beta invitees from tweet replies + Indie Hackers community; mechanics beyond that NOT DISCLOSED.
- **mainDistribution**: X/Twitter build-in-public (Yannick's viral threads as living endorsement) + founder demo calls.
- **techStack**: NOT DISCLOSED in sources reviewed.
- **zeroUpfront**: NO for an X-first clone today: X API paywall ($100-$5k+/mo tiers, Enterprise ~$42k/mo) kills zero-cost start; POSSIBLY for non-X networks.
- **mrr100**: HIGH: proven $100 MRR path (beta list to paid in weeks); today's API costs are the blocker, not demand.
- **learn**: Sell the growth outcome, not scheduling; a demand-proving tweet can replace validation decks; reinvest early revenue instead of taking salary.
- **notCopy**: Do not clone a general scheduler head-on; do not depend on a single platform API without an exit plan (see Blackmagic, Friends+Me).
