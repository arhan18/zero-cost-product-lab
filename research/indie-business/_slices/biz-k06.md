- **productSolution**: Simple, reliable podcast hosting with unlimited shows, strong analytics, private podcasting, and human support from a calm indie team.
- **coreFeature**: Unlimited shows per account, IAB-style analytics, private feeds, dynamic ads, built-in websites, API.

- **freeTier**: No free tier; all plans start with paid subscription after trial.
- **trial**: 14-day free trial on all plans, no credit card friction stated.
- **revenueEvidence**: Founder-disclosed early MRR ($781 at launch, $4k Dec 2018); current revenue REVENUE NOT PUBLICLY DISCLOSED; team of six serving 30k+ podcasts implies multi-million ARR scale (INFERRED, not stated).
- **validationMethod**: Pre-sold early access: 51 paying customers and $781 MRR before public launch; validated via founder audience demand.
- **firstUserAcquisition**: Justin Jackson's existing audience (blog, books, MegaMaker community) plus Jon's podcast-tool users; Product Hunt launch Aug 1 2018.
- **first100Users**: Justin announced to his MegaMaker/blog audience and X followers; 51 early-access customers pre-launch; launch-week hustle documented on Build Your SaaS eps 22-30.
- **mainDistribution**: Founder audience + Build Your SaaS build-in-public podcast + content/SEO engine.
- **techStack**: Web app + RSS/analytics pipeline; exact stack NOT PUBLICLY DISCLOSED (founder Rails-background context only; do not assert).
- **zeroUpfront**: POSSIBLY: code-first SaaS with modest hosting costs; founders self-funded and kept day jobs until $20k MRR, so near-zero but not literally zero.
- **mrr100**: HIGH: $19-$99/mo self-serve with unlimited-shows wedge; reached team-sustaining MRR within ~1 year; solo founder could reach $100 MRR with a handful of customers.
- **learn**: Audience-first bootstrapping works: pre-built trust converts to first revenue; building in public compounds into distribution; saying no to oversized customers protects focus.
