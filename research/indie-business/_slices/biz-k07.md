## Planning Center (https://www.planningcenter.com/)

- **productName**: Planning Center
- **url**: https://www.planningcenter.com/
- **founder**: Jeff Berg and Aaron Stewart (co-founders; Jeff Spillman joined early as operations/culture lead)
- **teamSize**: ~100-250 (Crunchbase 101-250; Tracxn official 238); 75 staff in 2018, 8 at 2011 move to Carlsbad; 82% remote.
- **launchYear**: 2006 (built 2005-06 while on staff at Southwest Community Church; launched at Purpose Driven Worship Conference, Saddleback, on $10,000 of credit-card funds)
- **category**: Vertical SaaS / Church management (worship planning + full ChMS suite)
- **targetCustomer**: Churches of all sizes, from plants to megachurches, plus their staff and volunteers.
- **coreProblem**: Planning Sunday services and running church operations meant spreadsheets, Access databases, retyped orders of service, and chaotic volunteer coordination.
- **productSolution**: Modular suite (Services, People, Check-Ins, Giving, Groups, Registrations, Calendar, Publishing) plus free Church Center congregant app.
- **coreFeature**: Service planning with drag-and-drop order of service, auto-timing and one-click volunteer accept/decline scheduling.
- **initialMVP**: Drag-and-drop order of service with timing, built for their own church; then the accept/decline volunteer button that 'changed everything.'
- **featuresNotInMVP**: Giving, Check-Ins, Registrations, Calendar, Church Center app, Publishing; founders explicitly said 'We will never be a church management system.'
- **pricingModel**: Modular per-product subscriptions scaled by usage; no contracts; giving processing 2.15%+$0.30 cards, 0%+$0.30 ACH (US).
- **pricingTiers**: Each product has free micro-tier (e.g. Services 5 team members, Check-Ins 10/day, Giving 10 donations/mo) then usage-scaled paid tiers; People database free unlimited; Music Stand add-on $5/mo.
- **freeTier**: YES: People free unlimited; every product has a free tier; Church Center base app free; 12-month free Giving for church plants.
- **trial**: 30 days free on paid tiers, no fees; cancel/upgrade/downgrade anytime.
- **revenueEvidence**: REVENUE NOT PUBLICLY DISCLOSED; Tracxn ESTIMATE ~$30.8M (secondary estimate, treat with caution).
- **validationMethod**: Scratch-own-itch: solved their own Sunday-planning pain, then showed it at conferences and coded requested features same-day.
- **firstUserAcquisition**: Saddleback Purpose Driven Worship Conference launch (June 2006) plus church visits; founder: 'showing people at conferences and visiting churches to help them get set up.'
- **first100Users**: NOT PUBLICLY DISCLOSED; 'growing every week since' launch per 2018 founder interview.
- **mainDistribution**: Word of mouth + zero sales staff (founder: 'We are very proud that we have ZERO sales staff'); conference presence and church-to-church referrals.
- **techStack**: NOT VERIFIED from primary sources in this pass (commonly described as Rails monolith plus native mobile apps; treated as UNVERIFIED).
- **zeroUpfront**: YES (prototype: single service-planning module buildable with free tooling; distribution via own church network)
- **mrr100**: HIGH ($100 MRR ≈ 2-4 small churches on paid modules)
- **learn**: Scratch-own-itch plus founder-led conference selling can compound for 20 years without sales staff; free tiers per module beat freemium suite.
- **notCopy**: Do not copy 8-module scope at launch; start with one beloved workflow (Services) and expand only on demand.
- **evidenceGrade**: PRIMARY
- **sources**: planningcenter.com/about (bootstrapped, debt-free, founder-owned); founders' 20-years blog post June 2026 (MVP, conferences, independence); SDVoyager May 2018 Jeff Berg interview ($10k credit card, zero sales staff, bootstrapped); planningcenter.com/pricing (tiers, free plans, processing rates). Official about page names founders Jeff Berg and Aaron Stewart; founders' 20th-anniversary blog post (June 2026) signed by both; SDVoyager 2018 interview with CEO Jeff Berg confirms bootstrapped histo

- **url**: https://www.wildapricot.com/
- **founder**: Dmitry Buterin (founder/CEO 2006-2017) and Erin Sullivan (co-founder; VP Marketing)
- **teamSize**: Handful at start → 115 (2021) → 61 (PitchBook current); Toronto-based.
- **launchYear**: 2006 (first customer November 2006; BonaSource predecessor consultancy)
- **category**: Vertical SaaS / Membership management for associations, clubs, nonprofits, chapters
- **targetCustomer**: Small/mid membership orgs: associations, chambers, clubs, charities, nonprofits, local chapters.
- **coreProblem**: Small associations ran on spreadsheets + static websites + manual dues/event/email work with no affordable integrated system.
- **productSolution**: All-in-one: member database + drag-drop website builder + events + email + payments/dues + donations + online store + mobile apps, one flat price.
- **coreFeature**: Website + membership system in one: join/renew/pay online with automated renewals, reminders, directories and event registration.
- **initialMVP**: Handful-built v1: member database + basic website + dues/events for small nonprofits (first customer Nov 2006).
- **featuresNotInMVP**: Online store, mobile apps (admin+member), Personify Payments processor, chapter tooling, CommUnity/job-board/text add-ons, API/Zapier depth.
- **pricingModel**: Flat-rate by contact count; identical features on every plan; no setup/transaction/customization/cancellation fees (processor fees separate).
- **pricingTiers**: 100 contacts $66/mo monthly ($59.40 annual, $56.10 2-yr); scales by contacts (250/500/2k/5k/15k/50k tiers); 60-day free trial, no card; onboarding coaches free.
- **freeTier**: No free tier; 60-day full-function trial (account deleted if not converted).
- **trial**: 60-day free trial, no credit card; sales calls + Boot Camp education funnel.
- **revenueEvidence**: Founder-disclosed: 8-figure revenue ('one of them 8 figures (Wild Apricot)' — buterin.com); exact figure NOT PUBLICLY DISCLOSED.
- **validationMethod**: Consultancy-to-product: BonaSource client work revealed the repeatable small-association pattern; first customer within months.
- **firstUserAcquisition**: First customer Nov 2006 via founder/consultancy network (co-founder retrospective); early-detail NOT PUBLICLY DISCLOSED.
- **first100Users**: NOT PUBLICLY DISCLOSED.
- **mainDistribution**: Content/education engine (Membership blog, webinars, Boot Camp, ebooks, templates, research reports) + Capterra leadership + free-trial/onboarding-coach funnel.
- **techStack**: NOT VERIFIED from primary sources in this pass (cloud web app + mobile apps; stack NOT disclosed).
- **zeroUpfront**: YES (prototype: dues + member site buildable with free tooling; first users from consultancy clients)
- **mrr100**: HIGH ($66 entry → 2 orgs ≈ $132 MRR)
- **learn**: Consultancy-first discovery de-risks vertical SaaS: sell the service, productize the repeatable pattern, keep one price with everything included.
- **notCopy**: Do not copy delete-on-trial-expiry harshness without comms; expired-trial data loss stories travel fast in nonprofit circles.
- **evidenceGrade**: PRIMARY
- **sources**: buterin.com founder site (bootstrapped, never raised, 8 figures, 2006-2017 run); Personify blog Aug 2021 co-founder retrospective (co-founded 2006, first customer Nov 2006, #1 Capterra, 25k orgs, 115 staff); NonProfit Times Sept 2017 (22k orgs, 7M members, Personify/Rubicon acquisition); wildapricot.com/pricing (tiers, 60-day trial, no-fee schedule). Founder's own site buterin.com ('Wild Apricot... serves over 20,000 small non-profits... Each venture bootstrapped, never raised outside capital, Wild Apricot reached 8 figures'); co-founder Erin Sull
