6. Podcasts (17/100 — founder-story shows convert technical audiences).
7. Integrations/marketplaces (15/100 — AppSumo, AlternativeTo, app stores).
8. Product-embedded virality (booking pages, newsletter footers, scheduler credits).
9. Launch stacking (PH + HN + newsletter in sequence, never single-bang).
10. Audience reuse across products (Levels/Lou/Reimer/Dinh playbooks).
11. Cold/outbound done personally (k04 tools; ConfigCat's first $200 sale).
12. Partnerships with adjacent tools (15 directory/integration mentions).
13. Lifetime-deal communities (AppSumo as channel + capital).
14. Newsletter-as-moat (4dayweek lineage; k06 email tools).
15. Open-source distribution (GitHub stars → self-hosters → paid cloud).
16. Vertical networks (churches, gyms, groomers — k07/k08 first-10 pattern).
17. Review-site arbitrage (G2/Capterra/AlternativeTo listings).
18. Influencer equity boards (Taplio 17-influencer Creative Investor model).
19. Platform-risk channels (X API $42K/mo killed Blackmagic economics — channel as liability).
20. Paid ads only post-retention (absent as day-one channel in every success record).
