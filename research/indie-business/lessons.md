# Lessons (indie-business corpus)

What the lab should learn — and explicitly not copy.

## Learn

1. Compound part-time; the day job is the seed round (Gymdesk, HelpSpot, Invoice Ninja).
2. Niche-down wedges beat parity (SavvyCal-class logic repeats in every slice).
3. SEO owns durability (51/100) — ownable queries from day one.
4. Embed virality in the artifact (credits, footers, booking pages).
5. Boring stacks, solo-maintainable (zero microservices in 100 records).
6. Meter the expensive dimension (responses, events, minutes, renders).
7. Recurring or decay (ShipFast-class decay vs Gravity-class ladders).
8. Aggregators lie; primaries decide (GetLatka/Tracxn/PitchBook caught repeatedly).
9. Kill criteria need arithmetic (Max-MRR-ceiling logic; founder-verdict failures).
10. Patience is a strategy with dated reviews (Photopea-class; Phase 28 or zombie).
11. Pre-sell before building where possible (Chatbase 30-min first payment).
12. AppSumo is capital + learning, not just revenue (Plutio $250K week).
13. Vertical networks are distribution (churches, gyms, groomers — first-10 pattern).
14. Open-source is distribution with a paid tail (stars → self-host → cloud).
15. Maintenance-mode is a valid outcome (Numerous.ai) — not every product must scale.

## Do NOT copy

- X-API-dependent architectures (platform rent repriced to infinity; Blackmagic $500K→$128K).
- Inference-arbitrage wrappers without pricing power (Super AI).
- Consumer AI at subsidized unit economics (Vibe AI).
- Price-war positioning in commodity niches (Beam-class; Fathom premium wins).
- One-time pricing without a renewal story (ShipFast decay).
- VC-needing categories at indie scale (fiction-generation pace, 24/7 fleets).
- Unlicensed-content arbitrage (Voicify/Jammable IP takedown).
- Fake trust, invented metrics, aggregator-cited revenue (lab rules already forbid; corpus shows why).
- Launch-once-then-stop (single PH spike, no SEO follow-through).
- Building for "everyone" (unreachable audiences in every failed record).
