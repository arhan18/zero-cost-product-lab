# Pricing Patterns (indie-business corpus)

Models, tiers, free/trial structures (`pricingModel`, `pricingTiers`, `freeTier`, `trial`).

## Model frequency (/100)

Subscription 75 · freemium 22 · one-time 17 · free trial 9 · per-user 3+ · transaction/platform-fee 3 · lifetime 2.

## TOP 20 pricing patterns

1. Subscription default (75/100) — $9–$99/mo sweet spot for solo tools.
2. Freemium funnel (22/100) — free tier as acquisition, paid minority funds all (Tally-class math).
3. One-time desktop licenses (Sublime/Kaleidoscope/CodeKit lineage) — durable cash, weak MRR.
4. Tier-by-generosity (free / pro / team ladders with usage meters, not seats).
5. Metered-by-cost-dimension (responses, events, API calls, minutes — never seats where infra scales per-use).
6. Grandfathered early pricing (locks in loyalty, complicates later math — recorded in several).
7. Price ladders over time (Gravity-style $99→$397→$895 in sibling corpus; k03 $29→$49→$129 seats).
8. LTD then recurring (Plutio/Docsketch AppSumo → subscription conversion path).
9. Transaction/platform fees (Donorbox %, RunSignup, job-board posting fees).
10. Per-seat only where collaboration is the product (rare in solo-friendly niches).
11. Trial over freemium where abuse risk exists (AI inference: no free tier, trial-gated).
12. Annual discounts as cash-flow events (upfront collection funds development).
13. Add-on/overage discipline (metered overages beat plan proliferation).
14. Pricing-page contradictions as signal (ChurchTrac 3 prices; Hypefury page-vs-reviewers) — verify live.
15. $100-MRR math built-in (55 HIGH: $10→10, $25→4, $100→1 customer logic per record).
16. Platform-fee inversion warning (bootstrapped Teachery 0% vs funded Podia 5% — capital shows in pricing).
17. AI quota pricing (minutes/renders/notes pass inference cost with margin — required pattern).
18. BYOK as margin escape hatch (100% inference cost to user).
19. Donation/tip tiers (Bear Blog, Ko-fi Gold-era — community-funded tails).
20. Maintenance-update fees (desktop tail revenue — $29 update fees).

## $100-MRR relevance math (corpus: HIGH 55 / MEDIUM 16 / LOW 15 / UNKNOWN 14)

One-time-license businesses score LOW as MRR by construction — judge them as revenue milestones instead. Structurally unmonetized products (free, no IAP) cannot reach $100 MRR without adding paid tiers.
