# Revenue Evidence (indie-business corpus)

MRR/ARR/customer transparency audit. Founder-disclosed > primary reporting > aggregator ESTIMATE > REVENUE NOT PUBLICLY DISCLOSED. Estimates labeled, never stated as fact.

## Strongest paper trails (founder-primary, dated series)

- Healthchecks.io: live MRR $24K published (Sep 2026) + 5% donated to dependencies.
- Instatus: open page — $50K MRR, full stack, 67K users.
- Tally-class discipline echoes: Evaboot $0→$2M ARR (~4 staff); Podsqueeze $9K MRR @5mo → $16K @18mo; Chatbase $0→$10M ARR $0 raised; YourMove AI ~$30K/mo @80% margins (since sold).
- Gymdesk: solo nights-and-weekends → $3M ARR before 2024 PE.
- Taplio/Tweet Hunter: $339 MRR launch → 3,000+ payers in ~8 months; exit to lempire ($2M cash + earnout).
- NiceJob: 8-figure Paystone exit. Blackmagic: $500K offer rejected Jan 2023 → $128K fire-sale 4 months later (X $42K/mo API tier).

## Shapes

1. Slow-compounding subscription (HelpSpot 20 yrs, Tower, Dash).
2. AppSumo LTD as non-dilutive funding (Plutio $250K/week, $1M+ total; Docsketch $40K net) — with LTD-overhang drag afterward.
3. One-time desktop licenses (Sublime, Kaleidoscope, CodeKit) — durable revenue, weak MRR mechanics.
4. Transaction/platform-fee (Donorbox, RunSignup, job-board lineage).
5. Exit as return (Tweet Hunter/Taplio, NiceJob, ShortlyAI→Jasper, Elvanto→Tithe.ly).
6. Failure arithmetic: Super AI / Vibe AI postmortems (structural death with working products); Nudge Coach 14 yrs + $4.41M raised → shutdown Apr 2025.

## Aggregator warning (corpus-caught)

- GetLatka: VoilaNorbert founder misattribution (acquirer vs founders) + revenue self-contradiction ($6M→$1.3M); Repurpose.io $5M vs Prospeo $1.1M; Honeybadger "100% self-funded" vs Tracxn/PitchBook funding flags; Dante AI bootstrapped-claim vs $1.5–2M listings; Testimonial.to $2.4M vs frozen $400K ARR.
- Tracxn: ChurchTrac pricing pages contradict each other; Breeze user counts (10K vs 50K) on official pages; Wild Apricot $10K "seed round" denied by founder.
- Rule restated: aggregator = ESTIMATE floor, never a citation for decisions.

## Gaps

- Desktop tools (Tower, Dash, Fork, Charles, Sublime, Paw): absolute revenue NOT PUBLIC.
- Vertical SaaS: 8/10 in k07 founder-bucketed or UNKNOWN; tech stacks rarely disclosed.
- AI records: inference unit-economics disclosed almost nowhere except Formula Bot ($5K-bill lesson); pricing tiers decay fast.
