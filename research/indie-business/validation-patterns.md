# Validation Patterns (indie-business corpus)

How each product was validated — or wasn't (`validationMethod`, `firstUserAcquisition`).

## TOP 20 validation patterns

1. Personal pain / dogfooding (dominant: dev tools, Dead Man's Snitch, Healthchecks, SnippetsLab).
2. Internal tool pulled by clients/friends (agency-origin B2B: Bonjoro, Better Proposals, Dubsado).
3. Pre-sell / paid beta (Chatbase first payment in 30 min; $400 MRR in 9 days cases).
4. Waitlist + landing page (several k05/k06 records).
5. Customer interviews (HelpSpot 84-person launch list → $4K month one).
6. Manual service first (concierge → productized).
7. Audience-first (Taplio influencer board; Hypefury following; Levels-style).
8. Build-in-public (X threads as validation surface; Evaboot LinkedIn validation post).
9. Direct outreach / cold email (k04 outreach tools eating own dogfood).
10. Community post (IH launch posts, Reddit, niche forums).
11. Existing audience (podcast/newsletter holders launching tools — Transistor lineage).
12. Competitor weakness (vs Calendly/SurveyMonkey/GA — wedge positioning).
13. Repeated customer request (agencies asked for the tool version).
14. AppSumo as paid validation (Plutio $250K week = 10,000 paying validators).
15. Open-source adoption preceding monetization (Healthchecks, Invoice Ninja stars→customers).
16. Marketplace listing as test (AlternativeTo first funnel — ConfigCat).
17. Accidental demand (Bear Blog, Ko-fi — free thing, payment requested).
18. Employer-adjacent / consultancy clients first (k07 church networks, k03 agency clients).
19. No validation, survived anyway (several desktop tools — category ownership substituted).
20. Anti-validation (Nudge Coach: loved product, $4.41M raised, still shut down — validation ≠ viability).

## Warning

Launch popularity ≠ validation. Product Hunt spikes appear in 14/100 distribution stories but almost never as the first-users mechanism — first users came from audiences, lists, outreach, and communities.
