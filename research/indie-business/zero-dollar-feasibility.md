## The 7 NOs (why not)

- X-API-dependent clones (paywall $100–$42K/mo — platform rent repriced to infinity).
- GPU-heavy consumer inference at low prices (Vibe AI structural negative margin).
- Inference-arbitrage wrappers with zero pricing power (Super AI convergence death).
- Capital-scale fiction generation ($3M raised to hire pace — Sudowrite).
- 24/7 ops categories without revenue (PaaS-class uptime fleets).
- Techstars-then-scale paths (Hello Audio — prototype POSSIBLY, scale not $0).
- Disputed-funding cases (Dante AI — UNKNOWN pending primary record).

## Core distinction (every record)

Free-to-prototype ≠ free-to-operate-at-scale. Meter the expensive dimension from day one; platform rent can be repriced without notice.
