## i08 slice: SaaS Boilerplates / Starters / Ship-Faster Templates

**Research date:** September 2026
**Niche:** SaaS boilerplates, starter kits, and ship-faster codebases founders buy — micro-SaaS / indie SaaS
**Products researched:** 10 verified bootstrapped/indie products

## ShipFast (https://shipfa.st/)

**Evidence:** FACT — founder-reported revenue at every milestone ($6K/48h via newsletter Sep 2023; $123.4K May 2024, $18.2K Jul 2025, $8.8K Feb 2026 via income posts); Stripe-verified TrustMRR trailing-30d (~$2.8K, Aug 2026, via StealWhatWorks). 7 sources reviewed.

**Revenue:** Marc Lou built ShipFast in about a week in August 2023 after shipping ~16 startups in 2 years, and made $6,000 in its first 48 hours — more than all of 2022 (founder newsletter). A third-party case study puts the first five months near $250,000 with zero ad spend. Founder income reports trace the arc: $123,400 in May 2024 (~94% of his portfolio) → $18.2K in July 2025 (~37%) → $8.8K in February 2026 (~11%). Stripe-connected TrustMRR data (Aug 2026) shows roughly $2,800 trailing-30d — a ~98% fall from peak — while his newer products (TrustMRR ~$40K, DataFast ~$27K) carry the portfolio. Lifetime revenue passed $1M by end of 2024 (secondary review).

**MVP:** A week's packaging of his own repeat plumbing (Stripe webhooks, NextAuth, MongoDB/Supabase, Resend/Mailgun, Tailwind sections) as a Next.js repo with tutorials, sold to his existing X/newsletter audience. No funnel, no ads — "he had to open the buy button" (OperatorBook). Pricing: $199 Starter / $249 All-in / $299 CodeFast bundle, one-time, lifetime updates, unlimited projects, no refunds after repo access.

**Distribution:** X build-in-public (100K followers Jul 2024 → ~374K by 2026), Just Ship It newsletter, SEO course, 5,000+ Discord, $1,210 partner-discount bundle, Product Hunt Maker of the Year 2023. Notably, distribution did NOT collapse — audience tripled while revenue fell 98%.

**Takeaway for zero-cost lab:** ShipFast is both the beacon and the warning of this slice. Beacon: a week-long build to $100K+/mo on $0 ad spend, the purest zero-cost economics here. Warning: one-time pricing creates built-in decay (8,000+ buyers reuse forever, never pay again), and AI builders (Lovable, Bolt, Replit) now generate the same plumbing from prompts. Marc's response — bundle ShipFast into CodeFast at the same $299 — is itself the lesson: when the asset commoditizes, sell the education and the audience.

### Contradictions

- Starter Story triangulates ~$75K/mo (2026, 7 public sources) vs Stripe-verified TrustMRR ~$2.8K/30d (Aug 2026). TrustMRR is Stripe-connected and more credible for current state; triangulation likely lags or blends portfolio revenue.
- The $250K-in-5-months figure is a third-party case study citing founder posts, not an independent audit.

## Gravity (https://usegravity.app/)

**Evidence:** FACT — founder-disclosed revenue at four milestones ($2K/mo Jun 2019, $100K total Jun 2021, $25K/mo Apr 2022, $25K/mo Feb 2025) via Indie Hackers posts and founder site. 7 sources reviewed.

**Revenue:** Kyle Gawley (Northern Ireland, ex-VC-backed Get Invited founder) has bootstrapped Gravity since August 2018: ~$2K/mo baseline by June 2019 (128 customers, 90% margin — "enough to cover rent and eat out every day in Thailand"), $100K total across 27 countries by June 2021, $25K/mo by April 2022, still ~$25K/mo (~$300K/yr, profitable, solo) per the February 2025 Starter Story breakdown. Founder site claims $550K lifetime revenue, 1,200+ developers, 13K monthly traffic. Pricing moved deliberately upmarket over time: $99 (2018) → $397 (2019) → premium lifetime + $195/yr update renewals; the 2026 Next.js edition page shows $199 one-time.

**MVP:** A personal Node+React template extracted from repeated product setups (auth, Stripe, UI). Crude prototype + landing page posted on Indie Hackers at $99 — 3 copies month one, 5 the next quarter. Validation came from a coworking conversation (a dev noting boilerplates existed in other languages but not JS) plus IH/Reddit threads.

**Distribution:** Indie Hackers (first customers, ongoing), SEO (page 1 for 'node.js saas'), X (42,000+ followers), a demo-app email funnel ("what did you think of the demo?" next-day email), Trustpilot reviews, educational content. $0 on marketing, founder-stated — after paid tests "converted poorly."

**Takeaway for zero-cost lab:** Gravity is the premium-pricing masterclass of the slice. Kyle learned he was "seriously underpricing" and that raising prices attracted *better* customers who funded *better* support — the opposite of the race-to-$99 instinct. The $195/yr update subscription is the structural fix ShipFast never built: it converts one-time decay into recurring revenue. Eight years solo, 90% margins, no team, no office.

### Contradictions

- Launch year: founder IH post says bootstrapping since August 2018; Starter Story says Year Started 2019. Read as: built 2018, commercial traction 2019.
- Pricing varies by page/snapshot ($199 Next.js one-time vs $895 lifetime + $195/yr flagship vs $506–$2,995 aggregator range) — editions and dates differ; official page is authoritative for entry.

## Divjoy (https://divjoy.com/)

**Evidence:** FACT — founder-disclosed $16,265 best month ($10,452 PH-attributable, dev.to Feb 2021) and $2K MRR (Full-Stack Feed interview); $15K Startup School grant (founder year-in-review Jan 2020). 7 sources reviewed.

**Revenue:** Gabe Ragland (Y Combinator alum) launched the free React generator in 2019, turned on paid code export late that year, and bootstrapped to $2K MRR. Best month: $16,265 in January 2021 off the Product Hunt launch ($99 launch-day vs $149 regular FOMO pricing). Current revenue UNKNOWN; current entry price reported $199 (BoilerplateHub) to $299–$599 tiers (Boilerplates4SaaS) — official pricing not captured.

**MVP:** The only generator-style boilerplate in the slice: pick UI kit (MUI/Bootstrap/Tailwind), framework, auth, analytics → customize in a visual drag-in editor → export a complete working codebase or to CodeSandbox. HN launch day: 15K visitors, 5K codebase exports. Then Next.js + React Bootstrap support, component library growth from feedback, and a second PH launch.

**Distribution:** HN Show launch, Product Hunt (Oct 2019 #7 daily; Jan 2021 best month), deliberate relaunch cadence every 3–4 months, a self-described 90%-build/10%-community operating split evolving toward 50/50 with SEO + affiliates + paid experiments planned for 2021.

**Takeaway for zero-cost lab:** Divjoy proves the generator wedge: instead of selling one stack opinion, sell *choice itself* — the configurator is the product. The $15K Startup School grant is a replicable non-dilutive path for dev tools. And the operating split (90/10 → 50/50) names the exact transition every builder in this slice faces: building stops compounding without marketing.

### Contradictions

- Current pricing unconfirmed: $199 (BoilerplateHub) vs $299–$599 (Boilerplates4SaaS). Launch-era $99/$149 is the only founder-confirmed pricing.
- No contradictions on historical revenue; both figures are founder-disclosed and consistent (base ~$2K MRR, spikes on launches).

## SaaS Pegasus (https://www.saaspegasus.com/)

**Evidence:** FACT — $84.1K ARR 2023 via founder annual review (GetLatka); ~$8.33K/mo avg via Starter Story (Dec 2024); ~$105K ARR Dec 2024 is StartupFounderStories inference. 6 sources reviewed.

**Revenue:** Cory Zue (Cape Town, ex-CTO, also building scriv.ai) extracted Pegasus from his Build-with-Django content project and hit ~$10K MRR in ~2 years working a few hours a week alongside his job — "entirely passive" by his account. $63.2K (2022) → $84.1K ARR (2023) → ~$105K ARR inferred (Dec 2024). 1,854+ developers including YC-backed companies (homepage, Mar 2026); 5.0/5 Trustpilot across 40+ reviews. Pricing: free open-source tier as funnel; $249 Starter / $449 Professional (AI) / $699 Unlimited one-time; lifetime license, 1 yr updates, 7-day refunds.

**MVP:** A reusable Django skeleton from repeated builds (auth, billing, teams) productized for the audience he'd already compounded through Django tutorials. Customers describe life-trajectory-level impact ("my product never would've gotten off the ground without SaaS Pegasus" — six-figure-ARR founder).

**Distribution:** Own site + email list (the content flywheel), SEO, ritualized Black Friday promos (unlimited-tier-at-single-tier-price, then straight 50% off), testimonials, Slack community. Zero paid marketing ever, founder-stated.

**Takeaway for zero-cost lab:** Pegasus is the content-first blueprint: *years* of free Django tutorials became the launch list, the SEO moat, and the trust balance the premium price draws on. The free open-source tier is the zero-cost funnel done right — hobbyists self-serve free, serious builders pay $249–$699. And the HTMX-vs-React choice shows how to serve a boring stack's unsexy majority instead of chasing Next.js hype.

### Contradictions

- Founding year: first sales June 2019 (founder retro + Starter Story) vs GetLatka foundingDate 2017 — the latter is the earlier content era Pegasus was extracted from.
- $105K Dec 2024 ARR is computed (84.1K × ~1.25), not disclosed — treat as inference, not fact.

## Makerkit (https://makerkit.dev/)

**Evidence:** INFERENCE — founder-disclosed $8,606 Feb 2023 best month (IH milestone, Mar 2023); GetLatka $15.4K ARR 2024 is a low-confidence aggregator estimate; current revenue undisclosed. 6 sources reviewed.

**Revenue:** Giancarlo Buomprisco's (@gc_psk) full-time solo operation peaked publicly at $8,606 in February 2023 (+27%, 10K+ Google clicks/mo, 290 Discord users) within ~6–8 months of founding. GetLatka estimates only $15.4K ARR for 2024 — directionally inconsistent with an $8.6K single month, so the estimate is downgraded (methodology unverified). Current revenue UNKNOWN. Pricing (official, 2026): Supabase Pro $349/lifetime, Teams $649/lifetime, unlimited projects, Figma kit, Discord support.

**MVP:** A production Next.js + Supabase B2B kit — the most feature-complete in the slice: multi-tenancy, RBAC, super-admin, per-seat/metered billing, i18n, MCP server — sold as one-time license, expanded across Next.js → Remix → SvelteKit → React Router/TanStack Start.

**Distribution:** Indie Hackers monthly milestones in the open, SEO compounding (10K clicks/mo by month ~8), Discord community, documentation depth, X presence.

**Takeaway for zero-cost lab:** Makerkit is the cautionary counterpoint to ShipFast: the *richest* kit is not the *richest* business. Completeness (orgs, RBAC, admin) justifies premium pricing ($349–$649) and funds full-time solo building, but overwhelms MVP-stage founders — third-party reviewers explicitly route simple products to ShipFast instead. Lesson: match kit complexity to buyer maturity; B2B depth is a premium niche, not the mass market.

### Contradictions

- Entry price: official $349 Pro / $649 Teams (2026) vs $299/$349 personal + $599/$699 team (Mar 2026 review) — likely a price increase or variant timing; official page wins.
- HQ: LinkedIn says Singapore, GetLatka says Taiwan — founder appears nomadic; location uncertain.
- GetLatka's $15.4K ARR conflicts directionally with the founder's own $8.6K month; estimate disregarded as primary evidence.

## Wave (https://devdojo.com/wave)

**Evidence:** INFERENCE — product facts verified via official docs, GitHub (6,000+ stars), and 2026 review; no public revenue exists (free/open-source). 4 sources reviewed.

**Revenue:** UNKNOWN — and that is the honest finding. Wave itself is free and open-source (github.com/thedevdojo/wave); it monetizes indirectly via DevDojo Pro subscriptions (advanced video series) and the DevDojo media ecosystem. No revenue, customer, or conversion figures are public. It is the only free, full-*app* kit in the slice: auth, profiles, impersonation, Stripe/Paddle billing, roles, Voyager admin, blog, changelog, API tokens, themes, plugins.

**MVP:** A running Laravel SaaS app on first commit — register → verify email → subscribe → manage profile + admin — released as an open-source repo with docs and a sandbox-billing demo, maintained by the DevDojo team (Tony Lea; top contributors tnylea, bobbyiliev).

**Distribution:** GitHub (6,000+ stars) as primary discovery, DevDojo's tutorial audience, docs + video series (free intro, Pro-gated advanced), community PRs.

**Takeaway for zero-cost lab:** Wave is the template for the lab itself: *give away the kit, sell the ecosystem*. A free boilerplate is zero-cost distribution for paid education, memberships, and adjacent products (pairs with Laravel Spark $99/yr for deeper billing). For a zero-cost lab, this is the most directly copyable business model in the slice — the product IS the marketing.

### Contradictions

## Supastarter (https://supastarter.dev/)

**Evidence:** FACT — founder-disclosed $12K/mo average, 800+ customers at $349, ~3 years in (Indie Hackers, Aug 2025). 3 sources reviewed.

**Revenue:** Jonathan Wilke (German micro-agency background) productized his agency's repeat code, put up a simple site with a $49 Stripe link, and got his first sale within a week (2022). Almost three years later: $12K/mo average, 800+ customers, "one of the most popular starter kits for Next.js and Nuxt" — with zero launch fireworks described. Current pricing (official, 2026): Solo $299–$349 one-time, Startup $799 (5 seats + architecture call), Agency $1,499 (10 seats, client projects, white-label). Lifetime updates throughout.

**MVP:** Weeks of nights-and-weekends work packaging agency best practices (auth, payments, orgs, API, admin, emails, i18n, jobs, storage) with unusually down-to-earth docs. Dual-framework from early on (Next.js + Nuxt); now AI-agent-optimized (AGENTS.md).

**Distribution:** $49 Stripe-link validation before polish, agency client work as dogfooding, X testimonials and word-of-mouth, documentation depth, SEO, and consulting calls ($149 standalone) doubling as high-touch conversion.

**Takeaway for zero-cost lab:** Supastarter is the quiet compounder of the slice — no viral launches, no audience theatrics, just $12K/mo from docs + support + agency proof. Two replicable mechanics: (1) validate with a Stripe link *before* polish; (2) seat-based tiers ($799/$1,499) lift average revenue without subscriptions, sidestepping the one-time-decay trap that hit ShipFast. Agency-dogfooding ("we ship client work on this") is trust no landing page can buy.

### Contradictions

- Solo tier shows $299 vs $349 across snapshots (and $349 in the Aug 2025 IH post) — A/B or time variance; $299–$349 is the honest range.
- Site claims "thousands of founders" (2026) vs founder-stated 800+ customers (Aug 2025) — likely includes free/Nuxt users; customer count uses the founder figure.

## Shipped (https://shipped.club/)

**Evidence:** FACT — founder-disclosed $15K total revenue, 126 customers in under 4 months (blog + DEV, Mar 2024). Post-2024 figures unknown. 5 sources reviewed.

**Revenue:** Luca (building in public as @ikoichi; PH launch posted by Luca Restagno) launched Shipped on December 4, 2023 after shipping 6 side-project SaaS ($150K+ revenues) alongside his 9–5 since 2019. By March 2024: $15K total, 126 customers. Pricing reported $147–$157 (SaaS) and ~$207 (SaaS + Chrome-extension bundle) — though official copy simultaneously says one-time lifetime access *and* shows "billed yearly" text (unresolved; see contradictions).

**MVP:** Literal copy-paste of proven code from his own six SaaS (landing, waitlist, presales, auth via NextAuth + Supabase, Lemon Squeezy payments, affiliates, emails, Chakra/Tailwind UI kit, blog) plus docs and a Discord. Lean by design, with honest "roughly reuse X%" guidance for non-Next.js stacks.

**Distribution:** Build-in-public on X/Threads, SEO how-to blog (Schema.org, Sentry, VS Code, micro-SaaS lessons), Product Hunt (Jan 2024), Discord community, deep docs.

**Takeaway for zero-cost lab:** Shipped is the most replicable curve in the slice: $15K in <4 months as a 9–5 side project by extracting *already-profitable* code. The "extract from your own winners" path removes both technical risk (code is proven) and marketing risk (you sell to your own tribe). The Chrome-extension bundle is the slice's only distribution-widening move — sell the second launch surface, not just the second tier.

## SaasRock (https://saasrock.com/)

**Evidence:** FACT — founder-disclosed $4,380 MRR (Starter Story, Oct 2022) and ~$76K ARR with Gumroad/Stripe split (IndieHustle, Feb 2025). Consistent growth line via ~$6.5K MRR (Founderoo, Nov 2024). 6 sources reviewed.

**Revenue:** Alexandro Martínez (Zapopan, Mexico; ex-.NET/React) started SaasRock on March 19, 2022 — his *sixth* boilerplate iteration (prior SaaSFrontends made $765). Free 24-hour drop of v0.0.1 (1,066 downloads) → $299 overnight → 19 customers/$3,063 by end of April → $99–$149/mo subscriptions at June prelaunch → $4,380 MRR by October 2022 (~69 subscribers) → ~$76K ARR by February 2025 ($36,142 Gumroad/110 customers + $39,880 Stripe/53). Total costs: ~$300 domain + ~$70/mo hosting/DB. Bootstrapped, solo.

**MVP:** "The Remix SaaS kit" v0.0.1 ported in 7 days from his React/.NET kit — auth, subscriptions, audit trails, events/webhooks, plan builder, entity builder, blogging, permissions on Remix + Tailwind + Prisma + Stripe + Postmark + Supabase.

**Distribution:** The free-drop funnel (1,066 prospects in 24h off a 500+ like tweet), then a regimented multi-platform content blitz per launch (Indie Hackers, Medium, Dev.to, Hacker News, Hashnode, LinkedIn) + ConvertKit newsletter + YouTube "build a SaaS in 10 days" series, amplified by mentor Chris Kluis (who advised the rebrand + subscription pivot).

**Takeaway for zero-cost lab:** SaasRock is the funnel masterclass: a free drop converts Twitter attention into 1,066 *downloaded* prospects, and the price flip the next day monetizes the spike. Doing it on Remix — the non-hype stack — proves the playbook travels: unsexy stack + free sample + content checklist = $4.4K MRR in 6 months at $70/mo costs. Also the slice's sole LATAM founder story, and a pricing-journey warning (one-time → subscriptions → perpetual $1,399/$2,099 plans in under a year).

### Contradictions

- Post-v1.0 perpetual prices ($1,399/$2,099) were 2022 *plans*; current MVP/Core/Pro tier prices were not captured — marked UNKNOWN, not carried forward.

## Footer: Substitutions, Discoveries, Blind Spots, and Future Suggestions

### Substitutions Made
- **No substitutions were needed.** The brief fixed 2 products (ShipFast, Gravity) and left 8 open; all 8 first-choice candidates verified as real via primary sources (official sites + founder posts/interviews). Considered and deliberately skipped: **Jumpstart Rails / Bullet Train** (excluded by brief — already covered elsewhere); **CodeFast** (same founder as ShipFast — diversity preferred); **Laravel Spark** (official Taylor Otwell product, not indie-bootstrapped); **FlutterFlow** (VC-backed, fails the indie test); **Vercel SaaS-Starter** (big-tech free template, no founder economics to study).
- Stack coverage landed as: 5× Next.js/React (ShipFast, Divjoy, Makerkit, Supastarter, Shipped), 1× Node (Gravity), 1× Django (Pegasus), 1× Laravel (Wave), 1× Remix (SaasRock), 1× Flutter (ShipFlutter).

### Discoveries
1. **One-time pricing has a half-life.** ShipFast's 98% fall from $123K/mo was not a distribution failure (audience tripled) — it was structural: 8,000 buyers reuse forever + AI builders commoditize plumbing. Gravity's $195/yr update subscription and Supastarter's $799/$1,499 seat tiers are the two working antidotes.
2. **The free-drop funnel (SaasRock):** 24h-free v0.0.1 → 1,066 downloads → flip to $299 next day → first customers within 24h. The most mechanical launch play in the slice.
3. **Sell before polish (Supastarter):** a $49 Stripe link on a simple site validated demand in 7 days, two years before $12K/mo. Cheapest MVP test in the slice.
4. **Premium pricing as strategy (Gravity):** $99 → $397 → $895 deliberately, attracting serious builders who fund support. Opposite of commodity instinct, and it held $25K/mo for years.
5. **Content-first compounding (Pegasus):** years of free Django tutorials became launch list + SEO moat + trust balance; free open-source tier converts hobbyists to $249–$699 buyers.
6. **Give the kit, sell the ecosystem (Wave):** the only free kit, and the most copyable model for a zero-cost lab — product as marketing, monetize education/community.

### Blind Spots
- **Gender:** all 10 founders identified are male — documentation bias in the boilerplate niche, but unmitigated in this slice.
- **Geography:** 6 of 10 US/EU; representation from Mexico (SaasRock), South Africa (Pegasus), Germany (Supastarter), and probable Asia-nomadic (Makerkit) — better than i04 but still no Africa/Middle-East/SEA-native stories beyond nomads.
- **Revenue freshness:** only ShipFast, Gravity, Supastarter, and SaasRock have revenue verified within the last 18 months; Divjoy/Shipped figures are 2021/2024 anchors; Makerkit/ShipFlutter/Wave scale is estimated or unknown.
- **Failure cases:** all 10 are survivors. The burnout pile (abandoned boilerplates, post-AI-builder casualties beyond ShipFast's decline) is undocumented here.
- **AI-builder impact quantified once:** only ShipFast has hard before/after data on Lovable/Bolt/Replit substitution; the rest report positioning responses (AGENTS.md, MCP servers), not revenue effects.

### Future Suggestions
1. **Post-AI-builder longitudinal:** re-verify Gravity, Makerkit, Supastarter, Shipped revenues in 6–12 months to test whether ShipFast's 98% decay is category-wide or ShipFast-specific.
2. **Pricing-model shootout:** compare update-subscriptions (Gravity) vs seat tiers (Supastarter) vs perpetual (SaasRock) vs free-funnel (Wave/Pegasus) on revenue-per-customer with verified numbers.
3. **Failure slice:** document 5–10 abandoned or stalled boilerplates (dead GitHub repos, sunset announcements) to bound survivorship bias.
4. **Buyer-side verification:** interview boilerplate *buyers* (shipped vs shelved rates) — seller anecdotes ("launch in 7 days") are currently one-sided.
5. **Directory/AI-app starter extension:** cover the adjacent starters named in the brief (directory starters, AI-app starters) as i08-extension once 2+ primary sources each exist.
