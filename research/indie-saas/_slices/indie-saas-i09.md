## i09 slice: Job Boards / Remote-Work / Hiring Micro-Products by Indies

**Research date:** September 2026
**Niche:** Job boards, remote-work communities, salary transparency, and hiring micro-products — bootstrapped/indie
**Products researched:** 10 verified bootstrapped/indie products

## RemoteOK (https://remoteok.com/)

**Evidence:** FACT — founder-disclosed revenue at four milestones (~$10K/mo ~2017; $20,467/mo Feb 2018; ~$301K/yr Apr 2020; $65,651 Sep 2020); pricing verified live on official hire pages. 7 sources reviewed.

**Revenue:** Pieter Levels bootstrapped RemoteOK off Nomad List's audience and disclosed the whole climb: ~$10K/mo by ~2017, $20,467/mo (Feb 2018), ~$301K/yr (Apr 2020 HN comment), and a $65,651 single month (Sep 2020). Margins run 80-90% on one VPS. GetLatka estimates $3.4M for 2024 (secondary, unconfirmed) — no founder figure exists after 2020, so anchor on the $787K/yr pace and treat $3.4M as an estimate.

**MVP:** A list page reusing Nomad List branding, seeded by hand with jobs from Buffer, Sqwiggle, Zapier, and Baremetrics. Submissions started as a mailto: link, then a Typeform + payment flow at $200/mo. The yellow-highlight upsell (+$50) was copied straight from other boards. Technically trivial — one PHP file; the hard part was the audience, which he already had.

**Distribution:** Free cross-promotion on Nomad List's front page (random ad per page load); Twitter build-in-public with revenue screenshots; page 2 of Google within weeks; Product Hunt; word-of-mouth among remote startups. Zero ad budget, ever.

**Takeaway for zero-cost lab:** The purest audience-leverage play in the slice. RemoteOK proves the second product to an existing audience can out-earn the first, and that a single-file board with dynamic pricing ($299+ add-ons, auto-renew) compounds to seven figures solo.

### Contradictions

- GetLatka $3.4M (2024) vs last founder figure ~$787K/yr pace (2020). No disclosure since 2020 — treat the estimate as secondary.
- Tracxn geo-tags RemoteOK to China/Hong Kong — a data error; Levels is Dutch and nomadic.

## Nomad List (https://nomads.com/ — formerly nomadlist.com)

**Evidence:** FACT — founder-stated $20-40K/mo range, $336K/yr (Apr 2020), $30,416/mo slice (Feb 2018); $99 lifetime membership third-party verified. 7 sources reviewed.

**Revenue:** Levels described Nomad List at $20k-$40k/mo (~$300k/yr) with thousands of paying members, then ~$336K/yr at 972K pageviews/mo (Apr 2020) — noting COVID had halved it. With RemoteOK it generated ~3/4 of his >$1M/yr portfolio (Dec 2020). Secondary compilations put 2025 at ~$38-42K MRR. The throughline: a paid B2C community, not job posts, monetizes the free city data.

**MVP:** A public spreadsheet-style page ranking cities by internet, cost, weather, and safety — the ~7th project of the 12-startups challenge. Launched on Product Hunt and hit #1 on PH and HN "by accident." The Slack channel, added months later, became the monetizable layer.

**Distribution:** The accidental double-#1 launch; Twitter revenue screenshots; SEO across thousands of city pages; Slack word-of-mouth; nomad meetups. $65-$150 starting costs.

**Takeaway for zero-cost lab:** Spreadsheet-to-$300K+/yr solo on one VPS, running 10+ years with zero employees. The lesson: gate the community, not the data — free rankings are the funnel, high-trust paid membership is the business.

### Contradictions

- The live nomads.com homepage now shows a "Join free" CTA, contradicting the long-standing $99 lifetime membership — the model may have shifted to freemium; current pricing is UNKNOWN.
- COVID halved revenue (founder, Apr 2020) vs later recovery figures — different dates, no real conflict.

## We Work Remotely (https://weworkremotely.com/)

**Evidence:** FACT — Tiny Ltd Q3 2025 press release + MD&A filing (sale price, purchase price, dividends, IRR); $299 pricing verified live on homepage. 5 sources reviewed.

**Revenue:** No standalone MRR was ever disclosed (UNKNOWN), but the holding-company math is public and primary-sourced: Tiny Capital bought WWR in 2017 for $1.4M, collected $8.6M in dividends, and sold it Sep 29, 2025 for ~$9.8M — ~53% IRR, ~13x multiple. A plain $299-post board, held 8 years, printed cash the whole way.

**MVP:** A simple remote-only listings page spun out of the 37signals audience in 2013 (HN launch thread Nov 5, 2013) — the authors of REMOTE: Office Not Required selling picks-and-shovels to the movement they helped start. Single-price paid posts from early on.

**Distribution:** 37signals/Basecamp halo at launch; decade of SEO dominance on "remote jobs"; newsletter and alerts; Top-100-companies content; default-channel word-of-mouth. 6M monthly visitors, 41,900+ jobs posted (site claims).

**Takeaway for zero-cost lab:** The model matters more than the exit: one post price × volume × tiny team. And the meta-lesson — build the audience asset (book, blog, movement) first, then charge employers for access to it.

### Contradictions

- Tracxn's "Funding Raised" tag reflects the 2017 Tiny buyout, not venture funding — do not misread WWR as VC-backed.
- Headcount: PitchBook 5 vs Tracxn 29 (Dec 2024). Filing vs press release: $9.0M gain recorded vs ~$9.8M sale price — use $9.8M per official release, $9.0M noted.

## Remotive (https://remotive.com/)

**Evidence:** FACT — founder-authored Indie Hackers post ($12K/mo, Dec 2019), founder year-in-review (2x YoY, bootstrapped), founder .com-purchase post; pricing verified live on hire page. 6 sources reviewed.

**Revenue:** Founder-titled IH post "Bootstrapping to $12,000/mo as a Non-Technical Founder" (Dec 2019) plus a 2x YoY net-sales year with buyout offers declined. Current revenue UNKNOWN. GetLatka's $11M ARR / 111-employee claim is rejected outright (see contradictions) — likely a different "Remotive" entity.

**MVP:** A weekly remote-work newsletter run while Dutel (ex-Google, non-technical) worked in BD at Buffer — "the world's largest newsletter for remote workers" per a 2015 interview. A Gumroad ebook monetized early; the job board came later and was rebuilt in 2019 with a part-time CTO (a high-school friend).

**Distribution:** Newsletter-first (273K digest subs); multiple Product Hunt #1s; Indie Hackers transparency; SEO + Google Jobs; 320K LinkedIn followers; Slack community. Explicitly bootstrapped — "I'm not spending VC money."

**Takeaway for zero-cost lab:** The non-technical-founder blueprint: newsletter → curation → board, with profits buying the .com for five figures cash. Curation + newsletter is a defensible wedge against higher-volume aggregators.

### Contradictions

- GetLatka $11M ARR / 111-164 employees vs founder's indie narrative + Crunchbase 1-10 + official "indie team" page. Verdict: GetLatka unreliable for this record; founder sources authoritative.

## Levels.fyi (https://www.levels.fyi/)

**Evidence:** FACT — founders interviewed in Business Insider (bootstrapped, profitable since day one, hiring); official about page corroborates origin and model. 4 sources reviewed. Exact revenue UNKNOWN.

**Revenue:** No figures disclosed anywhere — marked UNKNOWN rather than guessed. Founder-stated mix: employer research reports, benchmarking subscriptions, job postings, a Pequity partnership, plus resume/negotiation services. "Profitable pretty much since day one" with 3 FTEs hired in one quarter (2021).

**MVP:** A crowdsourced site mapping Big Tech levels to salaries, seeded from friends' data after the founders saw a viral Google salary spreadsheet. ~95% crowdsourced, the rest W2/offer-letter verified. Side project 2017, full-time 2019.

**Distribution:** Word-of-mouth (the "overheard at an Amazon lunch" anecdote); Business Insider press; thousands of programmatic company/level salary SEO pages; newsletter, community, mobile app.

**Takeaway for zero-cost lab:** Monetize one dataset twice — free B2C lookups build the corpus, B2B benchmarking and coaching harvest it, and the coaching submissions feed the corpus back. Integrity ("never accept payment to adjust numbers") is the moat for a data product.

### Contradictions

- Team/reach figures differ across secondary sources (30 staff + 1.5M users vs "small team" + 3M+/mo) — different dates, both secondary. No conflict on bootstrapped/profitable status.

## Lemon.io (https://lemon.io/)

**Evidence:** FACT — founder's own site ($15M marketplace, Coding Ninjas origin), founder LinkedIn ($2.7M→$10M in 2021), official blog (bootstrapped, profitable, no VC, 40+ countries). 5 sources reviewed.

**Revenue:** Founder-stated: $2.7M to $10M during 2021, described on his own site as a $15M talent marketplace. Bootstrapped, profitable, remote-first, no VC. No audited current ARR — UNKNOWN beyond founder figures, but the three points ascend plausibly with no contradictions.

**MVP:** Started 2015 as Coding Ninjas — "Uber for web development" (next available pre-vetted dev picks up your project). Scaling that model "was a utopia," so Volodarsky (two failed startups behind him) pivoted to curated matching of startups with rigorously vetted engineers.

**Distribution:** Founder build-in-public (22K LinkedIn followers, free weekly workshops for $1M+ founders); SEO comparison content (Toptal vs Upwork); review platforms; case studies; startup referrals.

**Takeaway for zero-cost lab:** The indie marketplace path: start curated and profitable, resist grow-at-all-costs ("the moment I stopped trying to grow at all costs... everything changed"). Four-stage vetting with a 24-48h matching SLA is the product; $45-80/hr rates sustain margin without VC.

### Contradictions

- Tracxn's "168 employees" vs remote-first undisclosed core team — likely counts the contractor network; treat as rough. No revenue contradictions found.

## Dynamite Jobs (https://dynamitejobs.com/)

**Evidence:** FACT — $399 pricing verified live on official post-a-job page; founders' origin + 10x growth year on Startups for the Rest of Us; ManyPixels case study (customer-funded, ~20 remote team). 6 sources reviewed. Revenue UNKNOWN.

**Revenue:** No public figures — UNKNOWN, not inferred beyond noting $399/post × visible volume implies a mid-six-to-seven-figure run-rate (inference only). Verified: "grown 10x in the last year" (Rob Walling, Jul 2023) and customer-funded, founder-run status.

**MVP:** A board serving the founders' own Dynamite Circle need — hiring affordable overseas talent — launched ~2 years after Dan Andrews and Ian Schoen sold their 7-figure eCommerce business. Recruiting services ("Remote First Recruiting") were layered on as "beyond the job board."

**Distribution:** Eight years of free Tropical MBA episodes as top-of-funnel before the board existed; Dynamite Circle community; SEO; Slack support; 25%-off first post. The two-sided cold start was pre-solved by audience.

**Takeaway for zero-cost lab:** Textbook audience-first marketplace — the "hard to bootstrap" two-sided market IS bootstrap-able if you spend years giving value to one side first. Remote-first-only filtering (no hybrids) plus a talent manager per $399 post justifies premium over $299 rivals.

### Contradictions

- Team: ~20 (ManyPixels 2026) vs 48 (Tracxn Apr 2026). Tracxn also contradicts itself (unfunded vs "funded $*****") — aggregator noise; founder-run status authoritative.

## 4dayweek.io (https://4dayweek.io/)

**Evidence:** INFERENCE — revenue is a third-party estimate ($83.3K/mo, Starter Story) against a vague founder anchor ("more than my salary"); founding, build speed, subscriber counts are founder/primary-sourced. 6 sources reviewed.

**Revenue:** Starter Story estimates ~$83.3K/mo (~$1M/yr), profitable, solo — explicitly an estimate (Kaeda flags it "verified false" as founder-confirmed). Founder anchor: last year's revenue beat his ex-SolarWinds lead-data-scientist salary; ~$1K/mo around Feb 2022; full-time at ~18 months. The honest statement: solo, profitable, order-of-magnitude ~seven figures, unaudited.

**MVP:** A Webflow landing page with NO listings, posted to Hacker News to validate demand — 300 signups in 48h. Listings and newsletter came after; first revenue took ~6 months. Built in 2 days by a PhD in information retrieval who recognized real traction after ~10 failed side projects.

**Distribution:** HN validation; LinkedIn thought-leadership (primary channel after Twitter decayed); programmatic SEO on "4-day-week jobs"; 500K-lifetime newsletter; podcasts; SXSW 2023 panel. Crucially: job ads are NOT the main revenue — newsletter ads + affiliates are.

**Takeaway for zero-cost lab:** Narrowest-niche-wins: a 2-day build in the tiniest defensible niche, where the audience (500K subs) is the asset and listings are the side revenue. Automation-first solo ops; Google-algorithm dependence is the documented fragility.

### Contradictions

- $83.3K/mo estimate vs founder's vagueness — anchor on the founder, treat estimate as secondary.
- Founder says job ads aren't a main stream vs Starter Story's £299/mo framing — the business is audience-monetization, not board-monetization.

## Working Nomads (https://www.workingnomads.com/)

**Evidence:** INFERENCE — live board verified (30K+ jobs, premium, post-a-job); founding/ownership conflicted across sources; revenue and pricing entirely UNKNOWN. 5 sources reviewed.

**Revenue:** UNKNOWN — no founder statements, no filings, no credible estimates. Unfunded per Tracxn (secondary). Observable monetization: employer posts, seeker premium, sponsorships, alerts.

**MVP:** A curated remote-job aggregation site from 2014 targeting digital nomads — category × region × skill × salary taxonomy plus email alerts as the retention loop, premium subs layered later.

**Distribution:** Long-tail SEO taxonomy (dozens of category/region/skill pages); 343K LinkedIn followers; X/Facebook/Instagram; job-alert emails; aggregator reputation for "reliable" posts plus its own scam-alert content.

**Takeaway for zero-cost lab:** A durability specimen (12 years alive on aggregation + taxonomy SEO) but a cautionary record: verify who runs a board before citing it. Unresolved ownership caps the lesson at MEDIUM relevance.

### Contradictions

- Tracxn's "205 employees" vs its own 2-10 and LinkedIn figures — aggregator noise.

## CryptoJobsList (https://cryptojobslist.com/)

**Evidence:** FACT — founder blog (bootstrapped, full stats, journey), founder interview (weekend build), official X ($150/post), founder 2018 write-up ($4K/mo at $199/post). 7 sources reviewed.

**Revenue:** Founder-stated ~$4,000/mo at month 6 (May 2018) at $199/post with 215+ startups hiring. By Oct 2023: bootstrapped, 164K applications, 11K+ jobs, ~2.5K companies, through the 2020-22 boom into normalization. Current revenue UNKNOWN. GetLatka's $6.8M/297-staff estimate is rejected as implausible (Kaeda + headcount evidence agree).

**MVP:** A simple crypto-jobs list built over a weekend in 2017 while Shalupau (ex-failed-startup engineer, first BTC 2014) was himself job-hunting. ICO-flush startups flooded in; paid featured posts monetized from the start.

**Distribution:** Perfect-timing launch; Twitter job blasts (52K+); LinkedIn (84K); newsletter (57K weekly, 40% open); 491 partner Discords (214K members); PH/HN; Forbes/CNBC/CoinDesk/WSJ/NYT mentions. Transparency marketing via an open stats page.

**Takeaway for zero-cost lab:** Canonical "vertical exploding overnight — own the hiring channel" play: weekend MVP → $4K/mo in 6 months → still #1 after a full crypto cycle. The bidding-for-rank ad system prices attention instead of flat fees; the agency arm (success-fee only) harvests the high end.

### Contradictions

- GetLatka $6.8M/297 staff vs bootstrapped 1-10 narrative — rejected.
- $199 (2018) → $100+ bidding (2025) → $150 flat quote (2026): price evolution, not contradiction.

## Footer: Substitutions, Discoveries, Blind Spots, and Future Suggestions

### Substitutions Made

- **Homerun.co (Amsterdam ATS, €89-249/mo, 1.4K customers, 30+ staff)** — fully researched (official pricing/about pages) but CUT: acquired/funded status per Tracxn and post-seed scale weaken indie fit; an ATS is adjacent, not a job board. Kept as first alternate for a future hiring-tools slice.
- **No other substitutions** — all 10 records are real, verifiable, and outside the banned-products list. RemoteOK and Nomad List included as required (VERIFIED with fresh product records despite founders-corpus coverage).

### Discoveries

1. **The 13x job-board hold** (WWR via Tiny filings): $1.4M in → $8.6M dividends + $9.8M exit. Primary-sourced proof that boring boards compound.
2. **Audience-monetization inversion** (4dayweek.io): job ads are the side revenue; the 500K newsletter is the business. Most board builders optimize the wrong side.
3. **Bidding-for-rank pricing** (CryptoJobsList): $100-$2,569 attention auction beats flat fees when employer demand spikes cyclically.
4. **Gated-community > gated-data** (Nomad List): free rankings funnel, paid membership harvests — with a possible freemium shift now underway.
5. **Non-technical-founder path** (Remotive): newsletter → ebook → board → cash-bought .com, documented to $12K/mo on Indie Hackers.
6. **Integrity-as-moat** (Levels.fyi): "never accept payment to adjust numbers" + coaching-that-feeds-the-dataset flywheel.
7. **Aggregator unreliability as a finding**: GetLatka materially wrong on Remotive ($11M/111 staff) and CryptoJobsList ($6.8M/297 staff); Tracxn self-contradicts on Dynamite Jobs and Working Nomads. Founder sources win every tie.

### Blind Spots

- **Geography**: 7 of 10 founder bases are US/EU; only Lemon.io (Ukraine) and CryptoJobsList (Singapore) add spread. No LatAm/Africa/SEA-native boards — a documentation gap, flagged for a future slice.
- **Gender**: All identified founders are male — reflects sourcing reservoirs, not necessarily the market.
- **Revenue darkness**: 4 of 10 records (Levels.fyi, Dynamite Jobs, Working Nomads, WWR standalone) disclose no revenue figures; two more lean on estimates. Job-board founders disclose less than SaaS founders.
- **Failure cases**: All 10 survived; no dead boards (e.g., purged 2018-19 remote boards) were autopsied. Survivorship bias acknowledged.
- **AI usage near-zero**: Only RemoteOK's post-writing assistant documents any AI; the niche runs on curation, SEO, and email — AI-native hiring products are a future-slice topic.

### Future Suggestions

1. **Dead-boards slice**: autopsy 5-10 failed remote/niche job boards (shutdown posts, founder post-mortems) to price the failure modes this slice can't see.
2. **Homerun + ATS micro-tools slice**: Homerun plus other indie ATS/scheduling-for-hiring tools (already half-researched) as i10.
3. **Non-Western boards slice**: LatAm, African, Indian, and SEA-native job/marketplace products to fix the geography gap.
4. **Pricing-model comparison**: flat-post ($299) vs tiered ($299-448) vs bidding ($100-2,569) vs success-fee — match model to demand cyclicality.
5. **Revisit Working Nomads ownership**: confirm the Jay Kang / SEO Rank SERP LLC transition (or fork) before citing the record downstream.
