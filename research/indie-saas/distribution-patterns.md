# Distribution Patterns

How first users were actually found. Channel frequencies computed from `distribution` + `launchStrategy` across all 100 records (substring match, one record can cite several).

## Channel frequency (mentions / 100 records)

- SEO / search intent: **63** — the dominant durable channel (Photopea owns "photoshop online"; Tally owns form-intent queries; job boards own role+remote queries).
- Product Hunt launches: **30** — inflection events (Typedream #1 month, SavvyCal #2 month), rarely the whole story.
- X/Twitter build-in-public: **22** — Reimer, Lou, Levels, Duke; audience compounds across products.
- Founder blog / thought leadership: **18** — Duke (Applied Cartography), Levels, van Rossum.
- Newsletter cross-promotion: **15** — 4dayweek (500K list IS the business); "Built with Buttondown" footers.
- Podcasts: **14** — Indie Bites, IndieRails, founder AMAs.
- Affiliates: **13** — HeadshotPro affiliates alone do $50K+/mo.
- Hacker News: **12** — Photopea 2021 AMA ($1M disclosure doubled users in 7 months via Reddit AMA 2018: 1.5M → 3M).
- Integrations / directories: **10** — AppSumo/LTD launches (Keyword Sheeter lineage), listing ecosystems.
- Indie Hackers / Starter Story: **10** — discovery surface for founders, distribution surface for stories.
- Paid ads mentioned: **23** — mostly as "zero ad spend ever" (Photopea, Tally early) or post-traction scaling, not day-one.

## TOP 10 distribution patterns

1. **SEO as the compounder**: own 1–3 high-intent queries; free tool pages rank and convert for years.
2. **Launch stacking**: YC launch + PH + HN in sequence (Typedream, SavvyCal), not a single big bang.
3. **Build-in-public audience reuse**: each product launches to the last product's followers (Levels, Lou, Reimer).
4. **Product-embedded virality**: "Scheduling by SavvyCal", "Built with Buttondown" — every usage exposes the next user. Zero marginal cost.
5. **AMA-as-growth**: Photopea's Reddit/HN AMAs as the two biggest growth events in company history.
6. **Affiliate leverage**: HeadshotPro $50K+/mo via affiliates — pay only after revenue (fits $0 policy).
7. **Podcast credibility transfer**: founder tells the numbers story; hosts lend trust.
8. **Lifetime-deal kickstart**: AppSumo-style launches for cash + initial users (several i02/i04 records); watch for support-burden hangover.
9. **Integration parasitism**: distribute inside someone else's workflow (LogSnag, Bannerbear API, SnapshotOne API).
10. **Newsletter-as-moat**: 4dayweek, Morning-Brew-likes — list ownership beats algorithm tenancy.

## Anti-patterns

- Launching once and stopping (single PH launch, no SEO follow-through).
- Paid acquisition before retention is proven (absent from every success record here).
- Building for "everyone" — unreachable audiences; every record with traction names a findable first-10-users pool.
