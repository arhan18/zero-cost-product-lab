# MVP Patterns

How the first version actually looked across 100 indie products. Traced to records; `hundredMrrRelevance` in the dataset points at the same conclusions.

## TOP 10 MVP patterns

1. **Side-project compounding (dominant)**: Buttondown (4 yrs side), Tally, SavvyCal, Tally.so — years of nights-and-weekends before full-time. The MVP was allowed to be small because the day job paid the bills.
2. **Personal-itch origin**: Photopea (student project), GoatCounter, LogSnag, WIP.co — built for the founder first; audience found later.
3. **Niche-down wedge**: Buttondown (Markdown/API newsletters vs Mailchimp), SavvyCal (scheduling awkwardness vs Calendly), Senja vs Testimonial.to (same niche, different wedge). Win a sub-segment incumbents ignore.
4. **The 2-week build**: Buttondown's Django+Vue MVP in ~2 weeks; Chatbase first Stripe payment 30 min after posting pricing. Speed-to-first-dollar beats completeness.
5. **Unmonetized patience**: Photopea ran 5 years free before ads at 5K daily users. Monetize attention after compounding, not before.
6. **Dogfood-then-product**: Mailbrew, Sniptt, WIP.co — internal tool first, productized when friends asked.
7. **Launch-day mechanics**: SaasRock free-24h-drop (1,066 downloads → $299 flip); ShipFast launch stacking; PH #1/#2 placements (Typedream #1 month, SavvyCal #2 month) as inflection points, not starting guns.
8. **Deliberate non-building**: single-player-first (SavvyCal's Level lesson: avoid markets needing team adoption); no teams features, no mobile apps, no admin panels until revenue.
9. **Boilerplate leverage**: i08 slice — ShipFast/Gravity/Supastarter customers ship in days; the lab should treat starters as raw material, not product.
10. **Failure-shaped MVPs**: Splitbee (great product, acqui-hired — distribution ceiling, not product failure); Beam (cheapest-price MVP won users, lost the business); Launchaco (sunset/acqui-hired). MVP success ≠ business success.

## What was deliberately NOT built (recurring)

- Teams/collaboration (until single-player traction), native mobile apps, usage analytics dashboards, admin UIs, i18n, SOC2-grade compliance. Added only when a paying customer demanded it.
