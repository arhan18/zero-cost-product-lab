# Revenue Evidence

Transparency audit for the 100-record indie-SaaS corpus. Rule followed throughout: founder-disclosed > contemporaneous primary reporting > aggregator estimate > UNKNOWN. Estimates are bucketed, never stated as fact.

## Headline numbers

- **~54/100** records carry founder-disclosed or primary-reported revenue (ranges, MRR/ARR milestones, exit prices).
- **46/100** carry no verifiable revenue — marked UNKNOWN or estimate-only in `revenueEvidence`.
- Strongest paper trails (founder-blogged step by step): Tally.so ($0 → $4.3M ARR), Buttondown ($15K MRR 2022 → ~$630K ARR inferred 2026), SavvyCal ($10K → $20K MRR 2021), Photopea ($250K 2019 → ~$1M 2021 → ~$2.5–3M est. 2025), ShipFast ($123K/mo May 2024 → ~$2.8K/30d Aug 2026 — a documented 98% fall).
- Holding-company math (primary-sourced from Tiny filings): We Work Remotely — $1.4M in, $8.6M dividends + $9.8M exit (~13x).

## Revenue shapes observed

1. **Slow-compounding subscription** (most common): years as a side project, then full-time. Buttondown (4 yrs side), Tally, SavvyCal.
2. **Ads + premium split**: Photopea (~90% ads / 10% premium+B2B, 99%+ margin on ~$12.6K/yr costs).
3. **One-time pricing decay**: ShipFast — spectacular launch revenue, structural decay without recurring base (Stripe-verified TrustMRR).
4. **Marketplace take / transaction fee**: Gumroad, Podia (5% beginner fee), job boards (posting fees).
5. **Exit as the return**: Typedream (acqui-hired by beehiiv), Splitbee (Vercel acqui-hire), Saber Feedback (documented exit price).
6. **Newsletter-as-business**: 4dayweek.io — 500K newsletter is the product; job ads are side revenue.

## Aggregator reliability warning (evidence-backed)

Do not cite aggregators as primary. Caught in this corpus:
- GetLatka vs founder evidence: Oh Dear ($126.5K claimed vs ~$1M ARR founder-reported, 8x clash); Testimonial.to ($2.4M vs frozen $400K ARR since 2022); Remotive ($11M/111 staff) and CryptoJobsList ($6.8M/297 staff) both rejected; Podia mislabeled "bootstrapped, no VC" against TechCrunch $3M seed reporting; Frill CEO, FeatureOS founder, Nolt funding misattributed; Aragon AI called "bootstrapped" while its own blog names Neo/Sequoia Scout investors.
- Tracxn self-contradicts on two i09 records; MakerStack vs primary site on GoatCounter's paid tier (left UNVERIFIED).

## Gaps

- Stale figures: Divjoy (2021), Shipped (2024), Makerkit/ShipFlutter/Wave (none), Testimonial.to (nothing since 2022), Podia/Memberful (never disclosed).
- Job-board founders disclose less than SaaS founders (4/10 in i09 disclose nothing).
- Failed-product revenue (what dying looks like in numbers) exists only for ShipFast, Beam, Splitbee.
