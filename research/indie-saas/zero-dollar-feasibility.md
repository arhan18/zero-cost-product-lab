# Zero-Dollar Feasibility

Which niches a $0 lab can actually enter, grounded in corpus cost evidence. Verdict scale: ENTER (proven at ≈$0) / ENTER-CAREFUL (feasible with caps) / AVOID (structural cost).

## Proven cost anchors

- Photopea: ~$12.6K/yr total ($20 domain + $600 hosting + ~$12K AI features) at ~$3M revenue — 99%+ margin, solo.
- SaasRock: $70/mo costs → ~$76K ARR.
- Tally: ~2% of 800K users pay; free tier funded by the paid minority on commodity hosting.
- Static/SEO plays (job boards, directories, calculators): domain + free-tier hosting ≈ $20/yr.

## Niche verdicts

| Niche | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| SEO content tools / calculators | ENTER | Static-first, rank-and-compound, ad/affiliate monetization |
| Forms / feedback widgets | ENTER-CAREFUL | Free-tier abuse risk; meter by responses, not seats |
| Privacy analytics | ENTER-CAREFUL | Aggregation compute scales with traffic; price premium (Fathom) beats price war (Beam died) |
| Boilerplates / templates | ENTER | Zero marginal cost per sale; one-time pricing decays — plan recurring |
| Job boards (niche) | ENTER | Posting fees fund hosting; newsletter doubles as moat |
| Testimonial / social-proof tools | ENTER | Tiny infra, widget distribution |
| Education / courses | ENTER-CAREFUL | Video bandwidth is the cost; text-first keeps $0 |
| AI wrappers (text) | ENTER-CAREFUL | API passthrough costs per call — require pay-per-use margin from day one |
| AI wrappers (image/video/GPU) | AVOID | GPU bills need scale capital; only Levels-scale operators disclose surviving them |
| Realtime / collab / mobile-first | AVOID | Infra + complexity exceed solo $0 maintenance budget |

## Rules derived

1. Never subsidize usage: free tier metered by the expensive dimension (responses, events, API calls).
2. Premium pricing beats price war in commodity niches (Fathom vs Beam).
3. Recurring beats one-time for survival (ShipFast decay vs Gravity's $195/yr updates).
4. Affiliates/rev-share only — pay after revenue, per ZERO-COST-POLICY.
