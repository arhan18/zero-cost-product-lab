# Template — Product Brand Kit

Copy into `products/<slug>/brand/` (ENGINEERING-STACK §8). Prefer reproducible/code-generated assets and free/open assets. Every social/OG asset generated from the brand tokens so it stays consistent.

## Asset checklist

- [ ] `logo.svg` · `logo-mark.svg` · `favicon.svg`
- [ ] `pfp-1x1.png` · `social-card-1200x630.png`
- [ ] `x-banner.png` · `linkedin-banner.png` · `og-image.png` · `email-logo.png`
- [ ] `brand-guidelines.md` (below)

## brand-guidelines.md contents

- **Concept** — one paragraph: what the brand feels like and why.
- **Typography** — families, roles, free sources.
- **Colors** — primitives + semantic tokens (mirror Design.md, no drift).
- **Spacing / radius / shadows** — values, referenced not duplicated.
- **Iconography** — style rules (stroke, corners, sizes).
- **Illustration / photography** — allowed sources (own screenshots, open-licensed); banned: stock clichés, unlicensed AI faces.
- **Motion personality** — matches Design.md §14.
- **Voice** — do/don't examples (mirror Design.md §2).

## Rules

- No asset ships that the product cannot legally use (licensed, owned, or open).
- Tokens live in exactly one place (Design.md); brand files consume them.
