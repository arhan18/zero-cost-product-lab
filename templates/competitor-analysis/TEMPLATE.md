## Synthesis

1. **Patterns worth borrowing** — record explicitly: `pattern: <name>, borrowed from: <competitor>`.
2. **The gap** — concrete, specific ("x users complain Y"), not vague ("could be better").
3. **Monetization landscape** — what prices the market will bear (FACT/INFERENCE).
4. **Why-would-anyone-switch** — honest list; if empty, flag the idea.
