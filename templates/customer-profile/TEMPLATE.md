## 1. Who feels this pain

- Segment definition (job title, context, tools they already use).
- How they spend their day; where they go for help (this dictates distribution, Phase 25).

## 2. Pain in their words

Quote verbatim, with attribution, e.g.:

> "I keep re-doing the export every week because it loses the formatting." — reddit r/XYZ, 2026-09-01

## 3. Evidence grading

For each conclusion: `FACT` (quoted user), `INFERENCE` (deduced), `HYPOTHESIS` (unverified), `UNKNOWN`, `CONTRADICTORY EVIDENCE`.

## 4. First-10-users hypothesis

Name the first 10 people/users the product could realistically reach (where we would find/contact them). Clear `FACT` vs `HYPOTHESIS`.

## 5. Frequency + urgency

How often the pain occurs and how badly it hurts right now (drive: they search, they pay, they complain in public).
