# Template — Product Legal Checklist

Used in **Phase 22** (human gate: owner review). Every launchable product completes what its actual behavior requires — nothing more, nothing real omitted.

## Checklist (check each; N/A with reason where inapplicable)

- [ ] **Terms of Service** — describes actual product behavior, pricing, cancellation.
- [ ] **Privacy Policy** — what is collected, where stored, retention, deletion path.
- [ ] **Cookies** — only if cookies/trackers beyond strictly-necessary exist.
- [ ] **Refunds** — only if charging; states the actual policy (incl. AI-compute no-refund rationale where true).
- [ ] **AUP** — where user-generated content, API access, or sharing exists.
- [ ] **Data disclosures** — third parties that touch user data (hosting, email, analytics, AI providers).

## Rules

- Written for the ACTUAL product — no fabricated claims, no "GDPR compliant" unless real (PRODUCT-GATES.md §6).
- Generated from free template sources, then tailored; human reviews any interpretation.
- Host/provider ToS honored at all times — $0 does not exempt ToS.
- Revisit on every pricing/data-flow change (Rules.md §14 trigger).
