# Template — Post-Launch

Used after **Phase 24**, feeding **Phases 26–28**. Most products live here: iterate, pivot, or kill.

## 1. Feedback log (Phase 26)
Quoted, dated, sourced real feedback → categorized: strengthen / fix / defer.

## 2. Metrics (Phase 27)
Weekly table: date, active users, MRR, churn/abandon signal, search appearances. Every number traces to a recorded event.

## 3. Iteration loop
- Next user-visible improvement (from real feedback, not vibes).
- Deferred-extras backlog review: still unneeded? delete.

## 4. Review decision (Phase 28)
On schedule / at kill criteria: **ITERATE**, **PIVOT** (to what emphasis), or **KILL** (archive — the lab learns from it).
- Decision outcome → dated memo in `decisions/`.

## Rule
Bias to kill unless traction is real. A dead product archived beats a zombie product maintained.
