## Sections

1. **Domain map** — vocabulary, players, how the space is framed.
2. **Problem shape** — what the problem actually is, for whom, how often.
3. **Existing solutions** — what people reach for today (tools, workarounds).
4. **Search / demand signals** — what people actually search/ask for (with sources).
5. **Blocks the "obvious" reading** — things that contradict the nice story.

## Evidence grading

Every claim must carry one of:

- `FACT` — primary source (user-authored post, official stats, observed behavior).
- `INFERENCE` — built from facts, not directly observed.
- `HYPOTHESIS` — untested guess.
- `UNKNOWN` — we do not know; say so.
- `CONTRADICTORY EVIDENCE` — sources disagree (record both).

**Triangulation rule:** any claim that will be used as the basis for a BUILD must come from ≥2 independent sources. One tweet/comment/blog/AI answer/competitor claim is never proof.

## Open questions

List what remains `UNKNOWN` and how we could resolve it (next source, next experiment).
